GM Notes
The Quest
The hook: who the player characters meet, what that person wants, and the thing it is all about. Roll the book's Quest table (one die of each type) or write your own.
NPCs to meet
Roll the NPC table (attitude, kin, motivation, profession, trait, name) — it adds an NPC stat block on the Monsters tab using the closest typical NPC.
Journey Legs
15 km a shift on foot, 30 mounted; two shifts of travel a day (a third is a forced march). Off the road the pathfinder rolls BUSHCRAFT each shift — a bane without a map, a boon with a spyglass, a bane in very difficult terrain.
The Adventure Site
The way in, what is inside, unexpected guests, a deadly challenge and the boss at the end. Roll the book's Adventure Site table or fill it in.
Rooms
Traps
Typical traps: spike traps on chests and doors, trapdoors, poison darts. Spotting one takes SPOT HIDDEN.
Leaving the Site
What happens if the player characters leave for at least a shift and come back.
Treasure
Coins, valuables (worth a set sum the players simply know) and gear. A short adventure usually holds 3–5 finds. The book's treasure cards aren't in the app — pick from the gear lists instead.
Maps
Monsters, NPCs & Bosses
Monsters attack from a D6 table (never the same attack twice in a row) and draw an initiative card per point of Ferocity. NPCs roll D20 under their skills; skills not listed are 5.
