Build an Investigator

Choose a role and specialty, then apply the playbook. It fills your starting actions, drives, gilded action, illumination keys, and lists your ability options — then follow the reminders to spend your creation points.
After applying: Actions — raise one 0-action to 1, then distribute 3 more (max 2 each). Drives — distribute 6 more points (max 6 each). Gild one extra action. Abilities — pick one role + one specialty ability.
