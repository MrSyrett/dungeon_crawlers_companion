Build an Investigator

Choose a role and specialty, then apply the playbook. It fills your starting actions, drives, gilded action, illumination keys, and default abilities — then spend your creation points.
After applying: Actions — raise one 0-action to 1, then distribute 3 more (max 2). Drives — distribute 6 more (max 6). Gild one extra action. Abilities — one role + one specialty.

Illuminated Worlds — Quick Rules

Action roll. Take d6 equal to your action rating (0–3). Spend a drive point (or accept help) to add +1d, up to six dice. Roll and read the highest die: 6 full success · 4–5 mixed success · 1–3 miss · two 6s critical.
Rating 0. Roll two dice and take the lowest — no crit.
Gilded. A gilded action replaces one die with a gilded die; take its result (even if lower) to refresh a point in that action's drive.
Resistance. For every 3 maximum drive points you have 1 resistance in that drive. Burn one to reroll dice equal to the action's rating.
Marks & Scars. Body / Brain / Bleed each hold 3 marks; the 4th drops you and becomes a scar — clear the track, note the scar, shift one action point.
