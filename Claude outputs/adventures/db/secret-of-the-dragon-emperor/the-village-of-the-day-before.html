Chapter 1: The Village of the Day Before

Seven centuries ago the sorcerer Kato the Curious betrayed the dragon emperor Eledain in a pact with the demon Ix for eternal life. Ix kept the letter of the deal: Kato's tower and the village of Blackridge are frozen in a sphere where the sorcerer's final hour repeats forever, ending each time as a flight of dragons burns everything to ash. Past an ancient warning barricade and the mallard scholar Canutus Boetii, the heroes step through the shimmering event horizon and cannot leave. To escape they must master the loop with help from the jaded outsiders Ukka and Thorendil, bribe the troll Gorm with Malyn's plum pie, use time-bending dragon dung to climb the tower in time, and then burn the contract, strike a bargain with Ix, or destroy him.
