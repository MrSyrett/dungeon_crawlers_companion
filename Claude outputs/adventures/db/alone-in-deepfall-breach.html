Chapter 1: Alone in Deepfall Breach

Legend says the demon lord Sathmog's spear tore Deepfall Breach into the Misty Vale, a wound of caverns, passages and ruins plunging leagues into the earth and glowing red from below. A lone hero sheltering from the rain in a nameless chapel at its edge meets Ingolfr Stone-Gaze, a scarred old wolfkin demon hunter whose stone eye sees through rock. He sends them down into the Vault of Many Paths on five linked missions: free the orc smith Gorum from cultists, seize the Worldbreaker Shard from the cult leader Arioch, recover the calling stones from a dead mage's tower guarded by a troll, gather sap from the Soul Tree past a minotaur and the Raven Sisters, and have Gorum reforge the shard into a weapon of light. Played with no GM: the fortune chart and tables decide what fate holds.
