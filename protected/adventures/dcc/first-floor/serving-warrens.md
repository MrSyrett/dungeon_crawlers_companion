# Serving Warrens — Worked Adventure (Floor 1, 1–3 sessions of 3–4 hrs)

*Toolkit reference: Neighborhood: Serving Warrens, pp. 39–48. Theme: Hackneyed Attempt for Backdoor Pilot. Table: 2–3 players. Three sessions (~3.5 hrs each) with cuts at the end.*

Bogbricks had a heartbeat, Prickly Pack Alleys had a grudge; the Serving Warrens have a **succession crisis**, and the players walk into it holding the crown. Everything in the Neighborhood is downstream of one fact: the old MisChief is dead, his heirs are dead, and three different rats think they're next in line. The adventure is a political thriller wearing a rat costume, and the question it keeps asking is "who do you put on the throne?"

---

## 1. The One-Paragraph Answers

**What's going on.** A Rat-kin clan tried to replace Goblins and Kobolds as the dungeon's default Mob, and it was working until their leader overreached and got himself and his heirs killed. Now the clan is in a civil war with the Goblins, the Neighborhood is four very different places (a battlefield, a freezing self-storage complex, cramped tunnels, a sewer), and everybody with fur wants the throne. The first thing the players notice: **a jewelled crown of Goblin bones on the floor in a pool of blood**, and every rat who sees them standing near it assumes they did it.

**Who's the Boss and what does he want.** The Interim MisChief is a nine-foot Rat Knight who never wanted the job. He wants one thing: **to not fail again** — he's guarding the old MisChief's illegitimate child, the Rat Bastard, and he'll die before he loses him too. **Weaknesses, as player actions** (the book gives none, so these are mine): *If they threaten or grab the child*, the MisChief stops attacking to shield him — one round of no attacks, and a very hard look from the table. *If they fight from behind cover* — the traffic light, the parking-garage pillars — his Traffic Dodger cars run in straight lines and never turn. *If they get the Rat-kin sleep potion into him* (a thrown flask on a Major hit, or dosed food), he loses a round per dose and all his Rage-style follow-ups. *If they bring the Rat King*, the three-headed ally heals them and hurls magic at him. And *if they talk to him about the child* (CHA vs 14+F, with the tails or the trophy-room note as evidence), he'll bargain: the child's life for the stairwell.

**How the Mobs interact.** Rat-kin vs Goblins is the war; the players can join either side or play both. Rat Brutes are volatile (Roid Rage on noise/light/insults) and charmable (Advantage on mind-control) — buy them with wine or cheese. Rat Shamans are fire-obsessed and their fire *hurts their own Chilly Goats* (×2). Chilly Goats are immune to cold, weak to fire. Fire-Fighters die instantly to water — and the Sewers are full of it. Hooligans heal Rat casualties, so kill the Hooligan first. **Levers:** a bucket of sewer water; a lit torch near a Goat; an insult to a Brute standing next to a Shaman.

**Clock budget.** Floor 1: 150 hrs. Budget **30 hrs across three sessions.** **Comfortable:** 10 / 20. **Push at 24:** the Rat Prince gets impatient and starts the ritual without the party — and without the live Shaman and Brute it needs, which means he'll come take *theirs* (or them). **Hard stop at 32:** the MisChief moves the child down the stairwell and the subway entrance seals. Pre-picked complications: **#2** (Bethany's birthday cake with six rat corpses — her first interaction); **#4** (Stinkin' Pete's dynamite-in-a-rat-head, "Goblover? Or Ratmeat?"); **#6** (a fire that empties the quarter of Rat-kin but enrages Shamans and Fire-Fighters) as the accelerator.

## 2. The Succession (decide before Session 1)

Three claimants, and the players will end up backing one:

| Claimant | Who | What they want from the party | Payoff at the Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Rat Bastard** | A frightened rat *child*, the old MisChief's illegitimate son, hidden by the Interim MisChief in the Sewers | Nothing — he's a kid | Spare him → the Rat-kin lay down arms and stop slaughtering Goblins; *Merciful (Rube)* → Gold Sequelbait Box. Kill him → *Bloodthirsty Butcher* → Silver Asshole Box, and a very quiet table. |
| **The Rat Prince ("Michael")** | A dirty Hooligan (L5) who claims descent from the *last* Rat King, the one the old MisChief overthrew | The severed tails of his parents (in the trophy unit, tied in a knot), plus a **live Shaman** and a **live Brute** for a ritual. He won't say what for. | The ritual makes him a three-headed **Rat King**: he fights beside the party, healing them and hurling magic. Also "direct access to a stairwell." Whether a Rat King is *better* than a rat child is the question the players get to answer. |
| **The Interim MisChief** | The Boss. Loyal, desperate, competent | Nothing — he wants them gone or dead | Can be bargained with over the child. |

**Bethany Dow** runs alongside all of it: a grieving Level 3 crawler who will die here unless the party talks to her **three times** before the floor ends. Her three scenes are planted one per session.

**Stinkin' Pete** (the Goblin Survivalist, Level 12) is the Neighborhood's chaos agent and the only one who knows the *Three Blind Mice* knock — which opens both the sleep-potion flagstone and the one Sewer hatch that leads to the Boss.

## 3. Scene Ladder

### Session 1 — "Wrong Place, Wrong Time"  (Discovery → Mastering; The Walls)

**S1-0 · Cold Open — The Crown** *(exploration → combat)*
*Tag: Discovery • The Walls • Combat // Moderate: Rat Shaman on a Chilly Goat + 1 Rat Brute (2p) / + 2 Brutes (3p)*

Read-aloud: *Bodies, both kinds. A goblin with a pineapple-headed pike through its chest still has both hands locked around a rat's throat. Ahead, drums, and the sound of a fight that hasn't finished. At your feet, in a pool that hasn't dried: a crown made of goblin bones and gems. A procession rounds the corner — a rat in bone-hung robes on the back of a horned goat, two rats the size of linebackers behind him — and every one of them looks at the crown, then at you.*

GM: No parley — they charge. Run it as the book's hook: the party is guilty by geography. Tactics: the Shaman opens with Mini Fireball from the goat's back (and remember the Goat takes ×2 from fire — a missed fireball that lands near it is a gift); Brutes Roid Rage at the first loud noise. **The lesson of the fight:** the Brute keeps swinging once after it hits 0% — warn them. When it's over, three-headed Rat-kin corpse nearby → the *real* MisChief died here, and the players are now, as far as the whole clan knows, regicides. ~40 min.

**S1-1 · The Walls** *(exploration)*
*Tag: Discovery • The Walls • Exploration // battlefield; a Weak Brute patrol (1/2) digging through corpses if they're loud*

Read-aloud: *Dark grey flagstone stained black-red. The corridors are wide enough for a war and were used for one. Somewhere ahead the battle is still going — the sound never stops the whole time you're in this quarter.*

GM: Texture: the dry coughs of Shamans, the scrape of a Murder Hole above (INT check to spot it and avoid a crossbow bolt from the ceiling). Things to find: the **Goblin side** of the war (a Weak Goblin squad who'll talk if the party isn't wearing rat blood — they know "the Bastard" was taken to the Sewers); a **saferoom**; Bethany's **birthday party** (S1-2). Plant: a Hooligan watches from a hidden passage and vanishes — that's Michael. ~20 min.

**S1-2 · Bethany's Birthday** *(npc — interaction 1 of 3)*
*Tag: Discovery • The Walls • NPC // Bethany Dow (Crawler #9,923,220, L3)*

Read-aloud: *Six Rat-kin sit around a table. They're dead — propped up, paws folded. At the head of the table a woman in a bank blazer unwraps a silver ring, sobbing with happiness, and thanks the corpse on her left. Then she sees you, and smiles like you're late for cake.*

GM: **Want:** for it to be a normal day. **Friction:** any mention of the collapse, or of Brian, and she stops hearing you. **Tell:** she keeps waving with her left hand, small, the way Brian did. She's L3, has real Spells (a Mage — GM's pick), and will follow the party if asked kindly; she'll die if left. **This is interaction one.** Let them have cake. ~15 min.

**S1-3 · Michael's Truce** *(npc)*
*Tag: Mastering • The Walls • NPC // the Rat Prince, as "Michael," Hooligan L5*

Read-aloud: *A rat waits outside the saferoom, sitting cross-legged, both hands up, a needle-thin sword on the floor in front of him. He's filthy even for a rat. "You killed the MisChief," he says. "No, don't — I don't care. I want to help you kill the next one."*

GM: **Want:** the throne. **Friction:** he won't say what the ritual does; he lies that it's "a blessing." **Tell:** beady eyes bulge whenever he says "help" *and* whenever he says "Rat Bastard" — the players should notice both. **Deal:** he'll reveal the sleep-potion flagstone (S1-4) now; he wants the **two tails** from the trophy unit, a **live Shaman**, and a **live Brute** delivered to the Sewers. If they agree or he thinks they will, he gives them the note from the dead rat in the Cold Halls entrance (or they find it there). ~20 min.

**S1-4 · Three Blind Mice** *(exploration / puzzle)*
*Tag: Mastering • The Walls • Puzzle // the flagstone; Rat-kin sleep potion*

Read-aloud: *A corner where the corridor dead-ends in nothing special: a flagstone identical to a thousand others. Michael said tap out a rhyme. He didn't say which one. Scratched very small into the mortar: three tiny stick-figure mice, and three x's for eyes.*

GM: Player-facing: they need to tap "Three Blind Mice" on the stone (let a player actually tap it on the table). Under it: the **Rat-kin Sleep Potion** — puts a Rat-kin to sleep for the rest of the floor and lets it be added to Inventory (this is how you *deliver a live Shaman and Brute*). Two doses. If they didn't meet Michael, Stinkin' Pete teaches the rhyme in Session 2. ~10 min.

**Break points, Session 1:**
- *Smaller Goal:* Michael's deal accepted (or refused) and the sleep potion in hand.
- *Cliffhanger:* the Cold Halls entrance — the dead Rat-kin with the key and the note: *"Took the Bastard to the Sewers… make sure the trophy room CD is still sealed, DIM!"* — as a Chilly Goat bleat echoes down a bright, freezing hallway.

### Session 2 — "Cold Storage"  (Mastering; Cold Halls → Tunnels)

Recap. Clock ~10 hrs.

**S2-0 · The Cold Halls** *(combat)*
*Tag: Mastering • Cold Halls • Combat // Moderate: 2 Chilly Goats (2p) / 3 (3p), then a Weak pair of Rat Brutes rolling up a door*

Read-aloud: *A lobby that was a self-storage office and then a tavern and is now a morgue. Beyond it, halls lit hospital-bright, cold enough to see your breath, lined with roll-up doors numbered in Roman numerals. The temperature drops another ten degrees. A steel door bursts outward.*

GM: Goats first — Icy Aura once every three rounds (15ft burst), horns and fangs otherwise. Fire does double to them; a Shaman's fire spell scroll, a torch, Stinkin' Pete's dynamite all count. Round 2, two more doors; Brutes join. **The lesson:** every closed door in this quarter might have a Goat behind it (the *Emptied Unit* trap) — let them start listening at doors. ~35 min.

**S2-1 · Unit 400 — "DIM"** *(exploration / puzzle)*
*Tag: Mastering • Cold Halls • Puzzle // electronic lock; the trophy room*

Read-aloud: *Unit 400 has a keypad and a hand-painted sign: CD. The note said "keep it sealed, DIM." The keypad has ten digits and a very patient red light.*

GM: Player-facing. "CD" and "DIM" are Roman numerals: **500, 1, 1000**. Inside: severed heads on upturned boxes — Rat-kin, Goblins, crawlers — all frozen; the **Rat-Snatcher 2000** (spring-loaded claw arm, double damage vs Rodent-type, must be used untrained); and in the place of honour, **two Rat-kin tails knotted together** — the Rat Prince's parents, exactly what Michael wants. Let a player who *doesn't* want to hand them over say so. ~15 min.

**S2-2 · Bethany's Tea** *(npc — interaction 2 of 3)*
*Tag: Mastering • Cold Halls • NPC // Bethany Dow*

Read-aloud: *A throw pillow flies out of an open unit and hits someone in the face. "Useless!" A vase follows. Inside, Bethany Dow is sorting a dead stranger's belongings into "keep" and "junk," and her eyes are a little too wide when she sees you. "Sit! I'll make tea. I — hm. I don't have a kettle."*

GM: She's angry now, at small things. Let a player redirect the anger: *what did Borant take from you?* If they get her to say Brian's name, she keeps it together. **Interaction two.** She can now cast something useful for them (heal, light, a shield) once — her first act of choosing to be here. ~15 min.

**S2-3 · The Tunnels — Cough** *(combat)*
*Tag: Mastering • Tunnels • Combat // Weak: Rat Shaman + 1 Brute (2p) / 2 (3p); round 2: Weak mixed Fire-Fighters (1/2) + Brute from a side corridor*

Read-aloud: *The Cold Halls end in a hole in the earth. Beams hold up a tunnel you have to stoop in, lit by one flickering bulb every fifty feet. Somewhere ahead, a long, wet, hacking cough — and then a second one, answering.*

GM: The Shaman coughs, appears with a Brute, and round two Fire-Fighters and another Brute flood in from the side to corral the party for a Firestrike. **Fire-Fighters die to water** — there's ankle-deep water in the sewer grate under the party's feet (a Fire Pit trap in reverse: kick the grate open and the Fire-Fighter that leaps up lands in it). Non-Spell weapons do nothing to them. The Shaman is the **live Shaman** Michael wants — the sleep potion is the non-lethal option; say it. ~40 min.

**S2-4 · Stinkin' Pete and "Comfort"** *(npc — Bethany interaction 3 of 3)*
*Tag: Mastering • Tunnels • NPC // Stinkin' Pete (Goblin Survivalist, L12) + Bethany*

Read-aloud: *Sobbing, then a scoff, then a high, gruff voice: "You want comfort? Here." A one-eyed goblin in a coat of goat-hide with a rat skull for a helmet is holding out a snub-nosed revolver to Bethany Dow, grip first, the way you'd hand someone a tissue.*

GM: The gun is **Comfort** — an Enchanted Snub-Nosed Revolver, fires without bullets, Fire damage, must be "reloaded" every six shots. If the party arrives *after* the word "comfort," Pete's gone and the gun's on the floor. If before, Pete is here and talks. **Pete — Want:** blow up rats. **Friction:** paranoid; he asks which side they're on and only continues if they say "not the rats." **Tell:** smells so bad the AI comments. He knows the MisChief and the child went through the **escape hatch** in the Sewers, and he'll teach the **Three Blind Mice** knock (if they didn't get it from Michael) *on the promise of no mercy for the Bastard*. Let them lie to him. **Bethany, interaction three:** with the gun in her hand and someone asking her what she'd shoot, she gets *angry at Borant* — she's going to make it. She asks the party to message her if they ever need help. ~20 min.

**Break points, Session 2:**
- *Smaller Goal:* the tails, a sleeping Shaman, Comfort in someone's hand, Bethany saved.
- *Cliffhanger:* a wall explodes (complication #5) — a Shaman fireball, and rats pouring through the gap as the session ends.

### Session 3 — "Three Blind Mice"  (Things Blow Up; Sewers → the ritual → Boss)

Recap. Clock ~20 hrs.

**S3-0 · The Dice Game** *(exploration → combat)*
*Tag: Blow Up • Sewers • Combat // Moderate mixed Rat-kin (2/3) rolling dice; Weak Chilly Goats (1/2) grazing; 1 Fire-Fighter patrolling — surprise round if they time it*

Read-aloud: *The sewer is a cathedral — fifty feet high, a ten-foot canal of brown water down the middle, catwalks either side, thin steel bridges. On the far catwalk a knot of Rat-kin roll knucklebone dice and argue. Goats chew something. A ball of living fire drifts around them in a slow circle, like a guard dog.*

GM: Stealth window: time the Fire-Fighter's circuit (Unopposed Stealth vs 11+F) for a surprise round; don't alarm the goats. If they wait and listen, the rats **argue about where the Bastard is** — "through the hatch, the one you knock on." The Fire-Fighter over ten feet of water is the joke: one shove. The **live Brute** for Michael is here. ~35 min.

**S3-1 · The Hatches** *(exploration / puzzle)*
*Tag: Blow Up • Sewers • Puzzle // one right hatch among many; the knock*

Read-aloud: *Ladders everywhere, up to hatches everywhere. The first one you climb opens on solid rock. The second won't open at all. Above the canal, Stinkin' Pete's voice (or Michael's, or the rats') echoes in your head: "the sacred rhyme."*

GM: Pick the hatch with Perception/Investigation (the one with fresh scratches and a child's bloody pawprint on the rungs). Knock *Three Blind Mice*. Wrong hatch or wrong rhyme: nothing, and time (−1 hr per attempt — the clock is the trap). ~10 min.

**S3-2 · The Ritual (if they're feeding Michael)** *(npc / exploration)*
*Tag: Blow Up • Sewers • NPC // the Rat Prince becomes the Rat King*

Read-aloud: *Michael takes the tails like they're a relic. The sleeping Shaman and the Brute he lays down on either side of him. He does not ask if you'd like to look away. When it's done there are three heads on one body, and all three of them thank you.*

GM: If the party balks *now* — good; let them. If they go through: the Rat King is a **Hooligan with three actions, heal and firebolt, 6 HB**, fights beside them at the Boss, and afterwards — with Michael on the throne — a stairwell "just for friends." Note what they gave up: two living creatures, drugged. Some tables want that on the record. ~15 min.

**S3-3 · B-B-B-Boss Battle — 10th and Baltimore** *(combat, boss)*
*Tag: Blow Up • Boss Chamber • BOSS // The MisChief (L7, 11 HB) + the child; + the Rat King if made; Comfort, the sleep potion, and cover are the tools*

Use the street intersection map (p. 38 works well) — a subway entrance, a traffic light, a parking garage, a public library, fake stars overhead.

Read-aloud (entrance): *Up the hatch and into the open air — a city street at night, high-rises, a parking garage, a library, a sign that says 10th & Baltimore, and stars that are very slightly wrong. A rat nine feet tall in red pants stands up from the curb. Behind his leg, a small furry face. Time slows. "One group seeks escape," says a voice from everywhere. "The other? Cold-blooded muuurderrrr!"*

Then the AI box (paraphrase it: desperate rat, giant mattock, don't laugh), and: **"I will not fail again."**

**Round-by-round frame:**
- **Round 1:** he leaps to the top of the traffic light and calls **cars** (Traffic Dodger — 30 ft line, straight, as many crawlers as it can). Anyone behind a pillar or the library steps is safe from cars; tell them the geometry.
- **Manhole covers** from range; the **Mattock** if you close. He's Large and a Knight — melee him with the Rat-Snatcher 2000 (double vs Rodent) if someone's willing to use it untrained.
- **The child:** he keeps the Bastard behind his leg. Any threat to the child → he stops attacking to shield him for a round. *Say nothing about this being a weakness*; let a player discover it and then decide whether to use it. That's the scene.
- **Sleep potion:** a thrown dose that hits (Major Fail on his Evade or an Amazing throw) → he loses his next round.
- **Comfort:** six shots of Fire, then a reload action. Fire ×2 to any Goat he calls in if you add them for a 3-player table.
- **Rat King present:** heals the party, firebolts the MisChief, taunts him about the throne — the MisChief will target the King first. Good; that's the tank.
- **Talk:** CHA vs 14+F with the tails, the trophy note, or an honest "we didn't kill him" → he'll bargain the child's life for the stairwell and go quietly. Some tables will want this ending. Give it to them; it's the harder one to earn.
- **Scale on the fly:** rout → a second car from the other direction; wipe → the child runs, and the MisChief chases *him* for a round.

**After:** the MisChief falls; the child shakes him and looks up at the party. **Spare him:** *Merciful (Rube)* → Gold Sequelbait Box; Rat-kin lay down arms. **Kill him:** *Bloodthirsty Butcher* → Silver Asshole Box. If they hesitate, the MisChief's last strength throws himself between them and the child escapes — the *Rat Regicide Rip-Off* achievement ("you don't *really* get a reward") is the AI's commentary on a job half done. Either way: **the subway entrance lights up — the stairwell.** Bronze Boss Box + Neighborhood Map, +1 Level each.

**Break points, Session 3:** the Boss; if time runs out at S3-2, the ritual's three heads are the cliffhanger.

## 4. Power Levels Used

| Card | 2 players | 3 players |
|---|---|---|
| S1-0 Crown | Shaman on Goat + 1 Brute | Shaman on Goat + 2 Brutes |
| S1-1 Patrol (if loud) | 1 Brute | 2 Brutes |
| S2-0 Cold Halls | 2 Goats, then 2 Brutes | 3 Goats, then 2 Brutes |
| S2-3 Tunnels | Shaman + 1 Brute; then 1 Fire-Fighter + 1 Brute | Shaman + 2 Brutes; then 2 Fire-Fighters + 1 Brute |
| S3-0 Dice | 2 mixed Rat-kin + 1 Goat + Fire-Fighter | 3 mixed + 2 Goats + Fire-Fighter |
| S3-3 Boss | MisChief (+ Rat King ally) | MisChief + 1 Goat called in |

**Rat Brutes at a small table:** the "one more attack at 0%" rule is a killer for two players. Announce it every time. **Fire-Fighters:** non-Spell damage does nothing — if nobody at the table has a Spell, put water within reach in every Fire-Fighter scene.

## 5. People (NPC cards)

- **The Interim MisChief** — L7 Neighborhood Boss, Large Rat Knight, red pants. Wants: to not fail again. Guarding the child. Bargainable. "I will not fail again."
- **The Rat Bastard** — a rat child. The rightful heir by blood. Does nothing but shake the fallen and look up.
- **The Rat Prince, "Michael"** — Hooligan L5, dirty, sits with hands up. Wants the throne. Needs tails + live Shaman + live Brute. Becomes the **Rat King** (three heads; heal + firebolt; ally).
- **Bethany Dow** — Crawler #9,923,220, L3, Human, a Mage with real Spells. Three interactions → survives and gets angry at Borant. Tell: the small left-hand wave.
- **Stinkin' Pete** — Goblin Survivalist L12, one eye, goat-hide armor, rat-skull helmet, dynamite. Teaches *Three Blind Mice*. Hands Bethany **Comfort**. Asks "Goblover? Or Ratmeat?"
- **The Goblin Survivors** — the other side of the war. Will talk if the party isn't blood-soaked. Know the Bastard went to the Sewers.

## 6. Secrets & Clues

1. **You didn't kill him, but everyone thinks you did.** The three-headed corpse near the crown is the old MisChief.
2. **"CD" and "DIM" are numbers.** Unit 400: 500, 1, 1000.
3. **The tails in the trophy room** are Michael's parents'. That's what he really wants.
4. **Three Blind Mice** opens the flagstone (sleep potion) *and* the one Sewer hatch that matters.
5. **Fire kills Goats twice; water kills Fire-Fighters once.** Shamans hurt their own mounts.
6. **A Brute swings once more at 0%.** Hooligans heal — kill them first.
7. **The Bastard is a child.** The MisChief will stop fighting to shield him.
8. **Cars go straight.** Pillars, steps, the traffic light's pole.
9. **Bethany needs three conversations**, not one rescue.

## 7. Rewards

- **Bronze Boss Box + Neighborhood Map (Serving Warrens)** + the **stairwell** (subway entrance); +1 Level each.
- **Rat Regicide Rip-Off** → nothing ("you didn't *really* kill a king").
- **Bloodthirsty Butcher** → Silver Asshole Box (killed the child).
- **Merciful (Rube)** → Gold Sequelbait Box (spared the child).
- **Rat-kin Sleep Potion** (2 doses) — sleeps a Rat-kin for the floor; lets it be carried in Inventory.
- **Rat-Snatcher 2000** — spring-loaded claw, ×2 vs Rodent, untrained.
- **Comfort** — enchanted snub-nose, no ammo, Fire, "reload" every six.
- **Michael's stairwell** and a Rat King ally (if the ritual is done).
- **Bethany Dow** — a Mage who owes the party (message her).
- Storage-unit junk: no weapons, but antiques, makeup, furniture — a Silver box if they find the one thing worth something.

## 8. Cuts

**Two nights:** S1 = Crown → Walls → Bethany's Birthday → Michael → Cold Halls fight → Unit 400 (break on the tails). S2 = Tunnels (Shaman + Fire-Fighters) → Pete & Comfort (merge Bethany's tea into this — two interactions become one long one; she still lives if it's a good scene) → Dice game → Hatch → Boss. Drop the ritual: Michael takes the tails and *promises* the stairwell, and the Rat King is a Floor 2 problem.

**One night:** Crown → Michael (he gives them the sleep potion *and* the rhyme) → Cold Halls fight + Unit 400 → Hatch → Boss. Bethany appears once, at the birthday, and it's up to the players whether she follows them out.

## Optional Texture

The Neighborhood's theme is "Hackneyed Attempt for Backdoor Pilot" — the showrunners are auditioning Rat-kin as the dungeon's new default Mob, and the players are the pilot episode. Have the AI slip into *network exec* mode: "Testing well with the 18–34s," "we may have to recast the child." If the players spare the Bastard, the AI announces the Rat-kin have been "picked up for a second season" — which is why they show up again in *Food is Love*.
