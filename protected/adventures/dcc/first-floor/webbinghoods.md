# The Webbinghoods — Worked Adventure (Floor 1, 1–3 sessions of 3–4 hrs)

*Toolkit reference: Neighborhood: The Webbinghoods, pp. 49–60. Theme: Swamp-filled housing developments and trailer parks with webs and mutated plants. Table: 2–3 players. Three sessions (~3.5 hrs each) with cuts at the end.*

This is the floor's *economy* Neighborhood. Every other first-floor neighborhood wants to kill the players; the Webbinghoods mostly wants to **sell them something** — protection, drugs, information, a stairwell rumor — and kill them only if they don't pay. So the adventure is built around deals: with the Bad Llama crime families, with the Croaker kingpin, with a Game Guide who's breaking the rules, and finally with a spider who can't be dealt with at all.

---

## 1. The One-Paragraph Answers

**What's going on.** Borant built a silk farm and staffed it with cow-sized Giant Spiders, then let a hydroponics accident turn the plants carnivorous and a llama crime syndicate move in. The result is a drowned suburb — houses, trailers, apartment towers, all overgrown, all webbed — where once a day something screams and then stops. Four quarters: the fogbound **Hole in the Web**, the classic-rock-blaring **Markets**, the spider-lair **Underwebs**, and the Croakers' secret spa, **Croaker Paradise**. The first thing the players notice: **a claxon, a glowing sign — WEB HARVEST IN PROGRESS — and combines the size of houses starting to move.**

**Who's the Boss and what does she want.** Aranaea Magnus, the Great Big Spider, wants **to eat, and to keep her babies and her pets safe.** That's it; she's an animal with a lair full of cocoons. **The weaknesses (these two are the book's, which is a gift):** *She tangles in her own webs* — a −10 Move penalty in webbing; lure her through it and she's slow enough to tie down (STR checks to bind legs). *She has three pet Slimy Croakers* — Piotr, Milo, Genevieve, bejewelled collars — and threatening them **distresses her so much she's easier to hit** (at the cost of her wrath). Two more from her stat block that the players can engineer: *she Drops at the start of a fight* — if there's Croaker slime on the landing zone she slips and loses **two Health Bars** on her own; and *if the Drop misses everyone*, she's Stunned for the round and loses an Action next round. So the whole plan is: bring a Croaker (or a jar of its slime), stand on the webs, don't stand under her.

**How the Mobs interact.** Giant Spiders and Slimy Croakers are symbiotic — Croakers guard the eggs; a Croak summons every Spider in earshot (and *damaging eggs* makes all Croakers within 60 ft swarm the attacker). Bad Llamas run the Markets and hate Vine Creepers and Spiders equally; Llama youth shoot pods for fun and displace Creepers into the fog. Literal Murder Hornets are contract killers who'll take a job from anyone. Vine Creepers are *plants* — they're triggered by vibration and buried in floorboards. **Levers:** a Croak as a Spider whistle; a coupon for Llama drugs as a Llama frenzy; Croaker slime as an anti-Spider floor wax; a hornet contract (payable in loot) on someone who deserves it.

**Clock budget.** Floor 1: 150 hrs. Budget **30 hrs across three sessions.** **Comfortable:** 10 / 20. **Push at 24:** the Web Harvest runs *again* — the combines come through whatever quarter the party is in. **Hard stop at 32:** Ribboff's stairwell tip is the only exit on the map, and it costs everything they have. Pre-picked complications: **#3** (two Llama families shake them down at once — turn them on each other) for Session 1; **#4** (Battle of the Bands — perform or fight both) as the Markets' set piece; **#5** (a delusional Giant Spider adopts them as pets, collars already made) as the Session 2 cliffhanger; **#1** (the double-wide pickup) any time the pace lags.

## 2. The Deals (decide before Session 1)

| Who | Offers | Wants | Where it pays off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alicia** (a Game Guide, breaking the rules) | A **Gold-rarity item** | Her favourite crawler, **Davy**, rescued from the Boss's lair | The Underwebs: Davy is in a cocoon in Aranaea's storage wall |
| **Ribboff** (Croaker "king," L10, non-combatant, Hawaiian shirts) | The **XP Chest** (one strike each; lowest roll gets +1 Level +1 Skill Rank), a **map to the lair**, and **stairwell information** of unknown reliability | To be cleared of a crime he didn't commit (the Croaker courtroom); cash or barter; not to be threatened (he calls **Dealbreaker**, an Ogress) | Croaker Paradise and the courtroom |
| **The Bad Llama families** | A denim jacket (safe passage in their trailer parks), key fobs for the alarmed cars (half of which are booby-trapped), "medicine" | Money, respect, and that you know the difference between Van Halen and Van Hagar | The Markets, the wedding, the Battle of the Bands |
| **Zach Nophobia** (Crawler #5,122, L8, Spider Slayer Supreme) | A fearless frontliner with a homemade swatter and a fan club | Company on a speed-run to bash spider eggs and stick Aranaea's head on his bat | Everywhere — and he'll get someone killed |
| **The Five Hanks** (Markets gate: Hank Spider, Hank Croaker, Hank Creepy, Hank Llama, Other Hank Llama) | Keeping the peace; information | Peace kept | The Markets |

**Davy** is the spine: Alicia's ask points at the Boss, Ribboff's map gets them there, and the Croakers (Ribboff's people) are how you beat her. Zach is the accelerator you deploy when the party is being too careful.

## 3. Scene Ladder

### Session 1 — "Web Harvest"  (Discovery → early Mastering; the Hole in the Web → the Markets' edge)

**S1-0 · Cold Open — Harvest** *(exploration → chase)*
*Tag: Discovery • Hole in the Web • Chase // the combines; a Weak Vine Creeper (1/2) and a Weak Giant Spider (1/2) displaced into the party's path*

Read-aloud: *A claxon. A sign burns in the air over the rooftops — WEB HARVEST IN PROGRESS — and from the fog comes a sound like a lawnmower the size of a church. Combines, a row of them, chewing through webbing and trailers and anything else in their lane. Something with too many legs bolts past you. Something green and enormous uproots itself and lurches the same way.*

GM: Keep moving. Each round in a combine's lane: 2d6 Bludgeoning (Evade 12+F to leap clear). The displaced Mobs are the fight — a Spider in the same panic as the players, a Creeper that grabs whatever's closest. Terrain: rooftops (safe from combines, not from Spiders), a flooded street (safe from Creepers' floorboard triggers, not from Spiders' webs strung between buildings — the *Spider Webs* trap: STR check, each fail sends vibrations). End when the combines pass and the fog closes in. ~35 min.

**S1-1 · The Hole in the Web** *(exploration)*
*Tag: Discovery • Hole in the Web • Exploration // fog; a field of pods (Mood #3); Llama youth shooting pods*

Read-aloud: *Fog, thick as wet wool, and when it thins: a subdivision that was airlifted here from six different states. A cul-de-sac. A trailer. An apartment tower on its side. Every yard has a mailbox and every mailbox has something living behind it. Somewhere ahead, teenagers whooping and the whump of fireballs — and the smell of diesel and petting zoo.*

GM: Three things: (a) **the pod field** (Mood #3) — stealth around them or fight; step on a vine and the Creepers wake; (b) **Llama youth** shooting Creeper pods for target practice — they'll sell the party a jacket, or they'll try the shakedown (complication #3: two families at once — play them off each other); *Green Thumb, Black Flag* is on the table if the players join in and destroy ten pods; (c) **the spray-painted arrows** — a scavenger hunt (fish a note out of a web sac; another from under a mustang's bumper) pointing to the **True Hole in the Web**, the neutral bar. ~30 min.

**S1-2 · Zach Nophobia** *(npc)*
*Tag: Discovery • Hole in the Web • NPC // Zach Nophobia (Crawler #5,122, L8) — the accelerator*

Read-aloud: *"NO FEAR! NO FEAR! NO FEAR!" Heavy rock from a boombox tied to a backpack. A man in a bike helmet sprints through the yards with a swatter the size of a canoe paddle, smashing egg sacs as he goes. Behind him, a dozen very interested spiders. He sees you, and his face lights up like you're his new best friends.*

GM: **Want:** Aranaea's head on his bat; XP; an audience. **Friction:** no self-preservation and no interest in yours; he will not wait, sneak, or listen. **Tell:** narrates himself in the third person for the feed. Use him three ways: as a *lure* (he'll draw every Spider in a quarter), as an *ally at the Boss* who dies gloriously if the players don't manage him, or as the reason the party has to run. If they ditch him he's fine — and shows up at the lair anyway. ~15 min.

**S1-3 · The Llama Wedding** *(npc → mystery)*
*Tag: Mastering • Markets' edge • NPC // a wedding party of Bad Llamas from two families; the poisoned punch*

Read-aloud: *Barbecue smoke, classic rock, forty llamas in denim jackets under a canopy of Christmas lights strung between two trailers. Two patches on every jacket — two families, one wedding, peace at last. Somebody hands you a plate and a red cup before you can say no. Then a llama in a tuxedo vest falls over face-first into the potato salad, and every glowing throat at the party turns toward you.*

GM: A murder mystery with a clock: both families are one wrong word from a fireball war, and the party — the outsiders — are the obvious suspects. **The truth** (mine, the book leaves it open): the punch was spiked with **hornet venom** by a **Literal Murder Hornet** hired by a youth from the *groom's own family* who wanted the feud back for status. Clues: a discarded stinger's venom sac in the punch bowl (Perception); a hornet's drone in the rafters (Perception — and it'll try to finish the job); a scorched patch on the youth's jacket that's *fresh* (he practised his alibi with lava spit); the hornet, if caught, names its client for a price. **Resolve it and keep the peace** → *There's a Llama and Another Little Llama* → Bronze Apparel Box (medicines + a **Llama Denim Jacket** — safe passage in the Markets). Blow it → both families blame the party. ~40 min.

**S1-4 · Alicia** *(npc)*
*Tag: Mastering • Markets' edge • NPC // Alicia, Game Guide, breaking the rules*

Read-aloud: *A woman in a Borant-blue vest sits on the tailgate of a wrecked truck with a clipboard she isn't reading. She looks up, checks something on her interface, and lowers her voice. "You're not my assigned crawlers. That's — fine. I'm not supposed to do this."*

GM: **Want:** Davy back. **Friction:** she can't be seen helping; she'll only talk where nothing is watching; she won't come into the Underwebs. **Tell:** keeps touching the AI-favor mark on her wrist. **Deal:** a **Gold-rarity item** for Davy, alive. She knows only that "the local Neighborhood Boss took him." If they need a way in, "the frogs know." ~15 min.

**Break points, Session 1:**
- *Smaller Goal:* the wedding kept peaceful, jacket earned, Alicia's ask accepted.
- *Cliffhanger:* the Markets' gate at night, the Five Hanks stepping out of the shadows, and Hank Croaker's slime already in the air.

### Session 2 — "The Markets and the Paradise"  (Mastering; the Markets → Croaker Paradise)

Recap. Clock ~10 hrs.

**S2-0 · The Five Hanks** *(npc / exploration)*
*Tag: Mastering • Markets • NPC // Hank Spider, Hank Croaker, Hank Creepy, Hank Llama, Other Hank Llama*

Read-aloud: *Five figures at the gate under a sign that says MARKETS in six languages. A spider. A frog in a bowtie. A vine creeper wearing a hat, somehow. A llama with a clipboard. And a man in a llama-print jacket who looks like he hasn't slept since the collapse. "Hi," says the man. "I'm Hank. We're all Hank. Business?"*

GM: Peacekeepers. Hank Llama enforces old customs (a lecture, and a fine, if broken); Hank Croaker throws goo in faces (Blinded, a minute) at any sudden move; Hank Spider "sneaks off" to string up troublemakers; Hank Creepy is inexplicably kind; Other Hank Llama is a crawler with Stockholm syndrome who'll tell them, if asked gently, that he's "glad he made some friends." They're the tutorial for the Markets' rules: no violence, cash only, don't lean on the cars. ~15 min.

**S2-1 · The Markets** *(exploration)*
*Tag: Mastering • Markets • Exploration // stalls, Seamus O'Spider's auction, the Battle of the Bands*

Read-aloud: *Miles of stalls in every direction, hand-painted prices, classic rock loud enough to feel. Llama stores, Croaker produce stands with slime still on the vegetables, and in the shadows between, small stalls selling paper goods and carved wood, run by hornets. From a stage in the middle, an amplified voice: "Lot forty-one! One rat, lightly webbed! Do I hear—"*

GM: Three set pieces, pick two: (a) **Seamus O'Spider's auction** — Mobs and, in private lots, crawlers; the party can *buy* a captive (rats, bunnies, a crawler who becomes a grateful NPC) or notice that the private-lot crawlers go to the Underwebs; (b) **the Battle of the Bands** (complication #4) — two Llama bands, and the party must perform (a real song at the table, or Performance/CHA checks with Disadvantage for anyone who won't commit) or fight both; a good performance earns a family's respect and the *Lava Baseball* setup — a llama tries to spit at them for fun, and knocking it back ten times over the Neighborhood earns a Gold Return to Sender Box; (c) **the car-fob scam** — a Llama family sells fobs for the alarmed cars; half are rigged to *trigger* the alarms and bring Mobs. A Lore or Streetwise check spots the tell. ~45 min.

**S2-2 · The Croaker Courtroom** *(npc)*
*Tag: Mastering • True Hole / Paradise entrance • NPC // Silas the interpreter; Ribboff accused*

Read-aloud: *A gavel. Forty frogs in silk shirts in a room that used to be a bowling alley, croaking at once. One frog stands apart, mouth working — and English comes out, thick with a lisp. "They thay Ribboff thtole the eggth. He didn't. He wath with me. You have to help him, or the thpiderth eat him at dawn."*

GM: Ribboff's been framed for stealing Giant Spider eggs (by a Croaker who wants his bazaar — GM's pick: **Genevieve**, one of the Boss's own pets, who resents that Ribboff is richer than the queen's favourite). Skill-puzzle it: 2–3 successes across Investigation (the eggs are in Genevieve's collar box), Persuasion via Silas, and a Croak-off if it comes to it. **Clear him** → Ribboff's deal opens (S2-3). Fail → the Spiders come for him at dawn, and the players can still rescue him for a lesser deal. ~30 min.

**S2-3 · Croaker Paradise and Ribboff** *(npc)*
*Tag: Mastering • Croaker Paradise • NPC // Ribboff, L10 non-combatant; the XP Chest; the map*

Read-aloud: *Under the streets, warm and humid and smelling like a pond in August: holiday lights, porcelain lamps, hot tubs full of frogs in silk. Rules on a chalkboard: NO WORK TALK. NO SPOILING THE MOOD. NO JUDGING BY SMELL. In a floral shirt at the back, a frog with the physique of a linebacker looks up from a burlap sack overflowing with loot. "Yo. You cleared my name. Let's talk about what that's worth."*

GM: **Want:** money, and to never fight. **Friction:** his information is a gamble — sometimes true; his clients "aren't seen again" (because they went downstairs — or died). **Tell:** he flexes when he lies. **Deals:** (a) the **map to Aranaea's lair**, with the pit-trap entrance marked; (b) the **XP Chest** — everyone strikes it once (Unopposed weapon Skill Check); the *lowest* roll gets +1 Level and +1 Rank; the rest get nothing — a great scene at a 2–3 player table; (c) **stairwell information** — GM's call whether it's true; (d) the fact that **Croaker slime makes spiders slip** — "why do you think she never comes down here?" — and a jar of it, for a price. Threaten him → **Dealbreaker** (an Ogress; run her as the Boss's stat line without the Drop) and you're out. **The Paradise is also a map cheat:** its tunnels cross the whole Neighborhood underground, no Spiders allowed. ~30 min.

**Break points, Session 2:**
- *Smaller Goal:* Ribboff's map and a jar of slime; someone leveled by the XP Chest.
- *Cliffhanger:* complication #5 — a delusional Giant Spider decides the party are its pet Croakers, shelters them "for the night," and has collars made. Roll commercials on the collar.

### Session 3 — "The Underwebs"  (Things Blow Up; descent → Boss)

Recap. Clock ~20 hrs.

**S3-0 · The Descent** *(exploration / traps)*
*Tag: Blow Up • Underwebs • Exploration // old webs + new webs; trapdoors; a Weak Giant Spider (1/2) on the ceiling*

Read-aloud: *Down. The walls are webbing — grey old strands over white fresh ones, so you can never tell which handhold will hold and which will hold you. Cars and motorcycles are wedged in the walls like flies. The floor is clear: someone's been sweeping. Ahead, faintly, a jukebox.*

GM: Every 50 ft, one crawler rolls (Athletics/Detect Trap) — a fail is the *Spider Webs* trap (stuck; vibrations; a Spider comes). Trapdoors drop into web-lined pits. The clear floor paths are the Croakers' — follow them and there are no traps. Play the tension, not many fights. ~25 min.

**S3-1 · The Croaker Bar** *(npc)*
*Tag: Blow Up • Underwebs • NPC // "Will Work for Snuggles"; the three pets*

Read-aloud: *A strip mall under a dome of web, and in what was a nail salon, a bar. Frogs on stools, webbed feet up. Three of them hold cardboard signs: WILL WORK FOR SNUGGLES. At the end of the bar, three more frogs in jewelled collars are being served first — and one of them is wearing a collar that's very slightly too new.*

GM: The unemployed Croakers (their Spiders died — Zach, probably) will **join the party** as companions for kindness; each one is a mobile puddle of anti-Spider slime, and a **Croak** is a Spider summons on demand — which is a weapon if you aim it. The three in collars are **Piotr, Milo, Genevieve** — Aranaea's pets, on a break. Kidnap one, befriend one, or just *learn their names*. (If Genevieve framed Ribboff, she knows the party knows.) ~20 min.

**S3-2 · The Storage Wall** *(exploration)*
*Tag: Blow Up • Underwebs • Exploration // cocoons; Davy; the tribute*

Read-aloud: *Spires of solidified web, skulls hung from them like lanterns. Outside the largest lair, a neat row of cocoons — tribute from the lesser Spiders, waiting for the frogs to carry them in. One of them is moving. One of them, when you cut it open, is a boy with a Borant wristband who says "Alicia?" before he passes out.*

GM: Davy is here, in the tribute row, not yet inside. Cutting him free costs time and a Stealth check (the cocoons are on her web — vibrations). Also in the row: a crawler from Seamus's private lots, and the **Silky Shirt** on a dead Croaker (incredible protection, ruined by slime — it's the *Spiders'* shirts that are wearable). If the party frees Davy and *leaves now*, they can — the Boss is optional, and Alicia's Gold item is theirs. Say that out loud. Zach won't leave. ~20 min.

**S3-3 · B-B-B-Boss Battle — The Feeding Ground** *(combat, boss)*
*Tag: Blow Up • Underwebs • BOSS // Aranaea Magnus (L7, 11 HB); pit-trap entrance; her pets; the webs*

Read-aloud (entrance): *The floor gives. You land in the dark on something soft, and then the walls light up — bioluminescent globes the size of beach balls — and the soft thing is a field of old bones and older cars. Colours bloom across the walls; a voice takes on the tone of a nature documentary about heroes; your faces appear, outlined in web, beside a silhouette with eight legs. Above you, something the size of a bull lets go of the ceiling.*

Then the AI box ("Our Boss Sense is tingling!"), and she **Drops**.

**Round-by-round frame:**
- **The Drop (round 0):** she lands on whoever's directly below. **If there's Croaker slime on the landing zone** (a jar thrown, a companion Croaker standing there, the party dragged a slimed cocoon in) she *slips* — **−2 Health Bars** on her own. **If the Drop hits no one** (everyone moved off the landing spot — say the globes' light shows where her shadow is), she's **Stunned** this round and loses an Action next. Either way, that's the players' opening.
- **Her tools:** Rending Leg-Claws (10 ft), Caustic Silk Spray (30 ft cone, Webbed + acid ticks; once/round), Venomous Fangs (Poison, −1 Evade), Web (90 ft, Held-ish: Disadvantage on next Evade, no Step until you pull free), Paralyzing Pedipalps (Staggered). She climbs walls and ceilings.
- **Her own webs:** every Web that misses **covers the surface** — the arena fills with webbing as the fight goes. She takes **−10 Move in webbing**. So: *let her miss*, then fight from the webbed ground; she gets slower every round. A STR check while she's tangled ties a leg (each success −1 to her Evade, cumulative — my rule).
- **The pets:** if Piotr, Milo, or Genevieve are in the room (the party brought one, or they came home), a *threat* to one — a blade at a collar — makes her **easier to hit** (Advantage on attacks against her that round) and makes her *furious* (she targets the threatener with everything). Actually hurting a pet: the Croakers in the bar never help the party again.
- **Eggs:** damaging eggs summons every Croaker within 60 ft *against* the party. Don't.
- **Zach present:** he goes straight for her head, gets Webbed round 1, and is the reason she's on the ground instead of the ceiling. Use him.
- **Allied Croakers:** they Croak (summons *other* Spiders — bad — unless the party needs a distraction), and they leave slime everywhere they step (good).
- **Melee warning:** her Pedipalps Stagger. Say it before the first melee attack.
- **Scale on the fly:** rout → a lesser Giant Spider drops from the tribute wall; wipe → she stops to cocoon someone (one round of no attacks) and the others can cut them loose.

**After:** Bronze Boss Box + **Neighborhood Map**, +1 Level each. *Friendly Neighborhood Exterminator* (Silver Pest Control Box) if anyone freed themselves from webs three or more times. Davy → Alicia → the **Gold-rarity item** (roll on the Atlas tables; let it be a good one). The Croakers in the bar: "so… who's *our* boss now?" — a Floor 2 thread.

**Break points, Session 3:** the Boss. If time runs out at S3-2, the cliffhanger is the floor giving way.

## 4. Power Levels Used

| Card | 2 players | 3 players |
|---|---|---|
| S1-0 Harvest | 1 Creeper + 1 Spider, displaced | 2 Creepers + 2 Spiders |
| S1-1 Pods (if woken) | 2 Creepers | 3 Creepers |
| S1-3 Wedding (if it blows) | 2 Llamas per family — flee | 3 per family — flee |
| S2-1 Battle of the Bands (if it fails) | 2 Llamas | 3 Llamas |
| S3-0 Descent | 1 Spider | 2 Spiders |
| S3-3 Boss | Aranaea (−2 HB if slimed) | Aranaea + 1 lesser Spider from the wall |

**Vine Creepers** are Huge with only 2 HB — the danger is the vine's pull (Held, pulled closer, broken only by killing it). Two players: never more than one Creeper at a time unless you want a TPK. **Giant Spider webs** halve movement and forbid Steps — the party will spend a lot of the Neighborhood at half speed; that's the theme, don't soften it.

- **Alicia** — Game Guide, rule-breaker. Davy's friend. Gold-rarity item for his rescue. Won't enter the Underwebs.
- **Davy** — the kidnapped crawler; in the tribute row outside the lair.
- **Ribboff** — L10 Slimy Croaker "king," non-combatant, Hawaiian shirts, burlap loot sack. XP Chest; lair map; stairwell rumor; slime. Bodyguard: **Dealbreaker** (Ogress).
- **Silas** — the only Croaker who speaks English (with a lisp); interpreter for **Stale Kale**, the Vine Creeper who sits on the Neighborhood's underworld council.
- **Zach Nophobia** — Crawler #5,122, L8. Fearless, reckless, a swatter and a boombox. Accelerator.
- **The Five Hanks** — Markets peacekeepers; Other Hank Llama is a crawler who won't leave.
- **Seamus O'Spider** — auctioneer; sells Mobs publicly and crawlers privately.
- **Piotr, Milo, Genevieve** — the Boss's pets, jewelled collars. Genevieve framed Ribboff (my addition).

## 6. Secrets & Clues

1. **The combines run on a schedule.** Harvest comes back at Push; rooftops are safe from them.
2. **A Croak is a Spider whistle.** Sixty feet, every Spider. Aim it.
3. **Croaker slime makes Spiders slip** — "why do you think she never comes down here?"
4. **She tangles in her own webs.** Let her miss; fight on the webbed ground.
5. **She Drops first.** Watch her shadow in the globe-light and don't be under it.
6. **Three frogs in jewelled collars** — Piotr, Milo, Genevieve. Threaten one and she comes apart.
7. **The Paradise tunnels cross the whole Neighborhood** with no Spiders allowed.
8. **Half the car fobs are rigged.** The family that sells them laughs a beat too early.
9. **The private lots at Seamus's auction** go to the Underwebs — that's where Davy went.

## 8. Cuts

**Two nights:** S1 = Harvest → Hole in the Web (pods + Llama youth) → Zach → Wedding → Alicia. S2 = Hanks (one minute) → Ribboff directly (skip the courtroom; he's grateful they're *not* here to rob him) → Croaker Bar → Storage Wall → Boss. Drop the Markets set pieces and the courtroom.

**One night:** Harvest → Zach (he has Ribboff's map already — stole it) → Alicia → Descent → Croaker Bar (a companion Croaker for slime) → Boss. Davy is the reward; the Markets are a rumor.

## Optional Texture

The silk is the point of the Neighborhood — Borant is *farming* here, and the crawlers are pests in a factory. Let the AI narrate the Boss fight like a corporate safety video ("Please do not damage company property"). And every day at the same hour, somewhere, someone screams and stops. If a player asks what that was, tell them: the harvest.
