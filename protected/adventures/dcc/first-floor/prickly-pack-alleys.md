# Prickly Pack Alleys — Worked Adventure (Floor 1, 1–3 sessions of 3–4 hrs)

*Toolkit reference: Neighborhood: Prickly Pack Alleys, pp. 29–38. Theme: Testing Bullshit Tolerance. Table: 2–3 players. Built as three sessions (~3.5 hrs each) with a one-night and a two-night cut at the end.*

Where Bogbricks was a place with a heartbeat, this is a place with a **grudge**. The Neighborhood's whole design is "the dungeon isn't fair" — so the adventure is built around three kinds of unfairness the players can *learn to beat*: getting lost (maps, Tracking, Terri's notebook), getting ganged up on (pitting packs against each other), and getting Washed (the Potion of Clarity, which is also the key to everything else here).

---

## 1. The One-Paragraph Answers

**What's going on.** A labyrinth of blue-green glittering rock: a few car-wide thoroughfares that curve just enough to fool you, and thousands of six-foot alleys that run for miles and spit you out ten feet from where you started. Packs hunt here — spined wolves, floating eyeballs that merge, half-cats that multiply, white rats — and the ambient sound is howling and, underneath it, **a buzzing that gets louder toward the Sprawl.** The first thing the players notice: they're already lost.

**Who's the Boss and what does she want.** Patti "the Bar Render" is a nine-foot retired public defender who bought a dive bar with thirty years of thank-yous and now runs it as a courtroom she finally controls. She wants **order in her court**: everyone seated, everyone charged, nobody leaving before the verdict. **The weaknesses, as player actions** (the book doesn't give one, so these are mine): *If they play along with the courtroom bit* — an "Objection!" backed by a CHA or Lore check vs 14+F — she is bound by her own theatre to *hear it*, and loses her next Fiery Cocktail. *If they order a drink* (step up to the bar, ask for something specific), the bartender in her serves it: one round of no attacks against that crawler. *If they kill both bailiff Barflies in the same round,* she must "call a recess" to summon the next pair — a round with no reinforcements. *If they light the bar* (thirty years of high-proof stock behind her; any fire source, including her own cocktails thrown back), the shelf goes up: 1d6 Fire per round to everything within 10 ft of the bar, her included, and she won't leave it. Terri Campion's Knight's Shield eats one cocktail a round if Terri is present.

**How the Mobs interact.** Canidnas and Pack Rats compete over corpses — an **Animal Handling** check can aim one pack at the other. Homogenous Humors merge if adjacent, so *splitting the party's targets* is the tactic; they're immune to ranged and blunt, so this is a spell-and-blade fight. Mirror Cats double every other round, so the Packrooms fight is a *clock*, not a battle. Barflies convert the dead; a Washed crawler who dies stands up as a Barflie with all their Skills — that's how the pickaxe murderer became one. The Naturalist mentioned in the summary never gets a stat block or a scene; I use him as a voice (see Optional Texture).

**Clock budget.** Floor 1: 150 dungeon hours. This Neighborhood is a *time thief by design* — the Topographical Disorientation trap kills maps for 30 hours, complication 1 drains 10–60% of remaining time. Budget **30 hours across three sessions** (~10 per session). **Comfortable:** 10 / 20 hrs at the session breaks. **Push at 24:** Barflies start dragging paralyzed crawlers past the party toward the Sprawl (complication 3) — a trail to the Boss whether they want it or not. **Hard stop at 32:** the door of Patti's bar is the only exit the minimaps will draw. Pre-picked complications: **#2** (rats fighting over a bloody will → good loot) for pace-lag; **#5** (the Kua-Tin corpse) as a planted scene in Session 2; **#6** (a rival party fighting over a Potion of Clarity) if the players lose theirs.

## 2. The Threads (decide these before Session 1)

Three NPC threads run through the alleys; each one pays off at the bar.

| Thread | Ask | Payoff at the Boss |
|---|---|---|
| **Terri Campion** (Crawler #13,123,444, L7, ex-cop, Knight's Shield + service pistol) | Find the crawler who killed another with an enchanted pickaxe. That crawler is now **the Barflie in the grave** in the Pack Alley Pall (Session 2). Bring Terri proof (the pickaxe) or bring her to it. | Terri takes them to the Boss Chamber and fights beside them — her shield absorbs one Fiery Cocktail a round. |
| **Jonai** (Gnome SYSTEM ERROR Master, L5, non-combatant, hiding in the Packed Halls saferoom) | Find out what happened to his apprentice **Xaggra**, who walked out toward a buzzing sound. Xaggra is half-Barflie in a Sprawl nest (Session 3); a **Potion of Clarity** saves him, but he attacks first. | If Xaggra is saved, the AI resolves the glitch by making Jonai and/or Xaggra the party's **Game Guide** — a permanent mentor. |
| **The murdered Kua-Tin** (a Borant admin, shot dead, no dot on the map) | The Game Guide hints Borant would pay goodwill to know who did it. **GM's choice:** (a) the pickaxe-murderer shot him with a stolen gun — clean, ties to Terri's thread; or (b) *Terri* shot him, because he was "doing something to" Jonai's contract and she "knows wrong when she sees it" — messier, better. | (a): Borant goodwill (AI Favor). (b): the players hold leverage over their ally in the middle of the Boss fight. Don't reveal (b) until they compare the bullet to her pistol. |

The Potion of Clarity is the Neighborhood's master key: it cures Washed, saves Xaggra, breaks the Topographical trap, and is the exit from the Packrooms. There are three in the Neighborhood (the *Another Round* achievement box, the tomb sarcophagus, the rival party's). **Make sure they have at least one before the Sprawl.**

## 3. Scene Ladder

### Session 1 — "Lost"  (Opening Discovery → early Mastering; Packed Halls)

**S1-0 · Cold Open — Six Ways In, No Way Out** *(combat)*
*Tag: Discovery • Packed Halls • Combat // Weak 1 Canidna (2p) / 2 (3p), +1 every round after the first — a chase, not a fight*

Read-aloud: *Six alleys meet in a star, every one of them black. The rock glitters blue-green in your torchlight like something wet. From one alley, a growl. From three of them, growls. Something wolf-shaped, its back a hedge of spines, steps into the light — and behind it, more eyes open.*

GM: One Canidna the first round, another joins every round after. The point is not to win: the point is **to teach the "keep moving" rule.** Pack Hunting: two adjacent Canidnas give the target Disadvantage on Evades. Any alley they pick is fine — pick the one that puts them on a thoroughfare, and let them hear the pack fall back with a last howl. If they stand and fight past round 4, a Canidna curls into a Spike Ball and that's the injury that makes them run. Ends with them "safe" — and with no idea where they are. ~25 min.

**S1-1 · Two Packs, One Corpse** *(combat / exploration)*
*Tag: Discovery • Packed Halls • Combat // Weak Canidnas (1/2) vs Strong Pack Rats (3/5) — pit them*

Read-aloud: *Around the curve, a dead crawler — and two dinner parties. A pair of spined wolves have his legs. A dozen white rats with red eyes have the wolves' dead packmates. Nobody has noticed you yet.*

GM: Each player gets one **INT-Opposed Animal Handling** check, choosing a pack to aim. Any success against a pack = that pack goes for the other one; no success against a pack = it comes for the players. All fail = everything attacks. Play the outcome as a three-way brawl the players can *stand back from*. The corpse: a blue dot fading; a crude notebook-paper map fragment in the hand (Terri's handwriting — the seed for S1-3); a Bronze box if they loot with style. ~30 min.

**S1-2 · The Maze Bites** *(exploration)*
*Tag: Discovery • Packed Halls • Exploration // Topographical Disorientation trap; no Mob count*

Read-aloud: *You pass the same cracked keystone for the third time. The alley mouths are starting to look alike — no, identical. Your minimap draws a corridor, erases it, draws it again. In the floor ahead, faint lines: a compass rose scratched into the rock, its needle pointing at you.*

GM: The trap. Only the crawler with the best Detect Trap/Perception rolls (Unopposed; "Totally Hidden" needs an Amazing). Triggered: the rune flares, minimaps scramble for 30 hours (or until sleep / Potion of Clarity), and the flash draws a **Moderate group of Homogenous Humors** from the ceiling (2/3) — remind them: ranged and blunt do nothing; keep the eyeballs apart or they merge. Avoided: they learn the Neighborhood's rule — **one Unopposed Tracking check per session keeps them un-lost; otherwise they draw their own map.** Hand them a blank sheet and mean it. Grinding Mood #2 is the texture here. ~30 min.

**S1-3 · Terri Campion** *(npc)*
*Tag: Discovery • Packed Halls • NPC // Terri Campion (Crawler #13,123,444, L7)*

Read-aloud: *A gunshot — flat, close. Then a woman's voice: "Stay where you are. Hands." She comes around the corner in the rags of a police uniform with a shield that glitters like a church window on one arm and a service pistol in the other hand, and she looks at each of you very carefully before she asks: "Have any of you been backwashed?"*

GM: **Want:** justice for a crawler killed with an enchanted pickaxe by another crawler who fled into the Packrooms and never came out the same. **Friction:** she doesn't trust anyone until she's checked their eyes for red; she will *not* hand over her notebook map — she'll walk them to the saferoom herself, or let them **copy it** if they're straight with her. **Tell:** calls everyone "sir" or "ma'am" and means it as a warning. **Deal:** help me find the killer and I'll take you to the Boss myself and hold the door. Plant: "There's a body in the Pall with no dot. A *fish* in a suit. Somebody shot him, and it wasn't me." (Or was it — see the thread table.) ~20 min.

**S1-4 · The Knight's Alley** *(exploration / puzzle)*
*Tag: Mastering • Packed Halls • Exploration // player-facing puzzle, 3 real minutes*

Read-aloud: *The alleys go quiet — no howls, no buzz, nothing. Then a smell, sharp and chemical, like a hospital corridor. It leads to a blank wall and a line of graffiti: "The Pawn may fear the King in his Castle, or the Priest on his Path, but safety is found in the Darkness of the Knight." Below it, a chalk drawing: a pawn facing down a rook, a bishop, and a knight.*

GM: Player-facing puzzle. Start a 3-minute timer. Answer: tap the brick closest to the **knight** — the square the pawn would be safe on if it moved there. Hollow: a fine **potion of Pack Repellant** (every Neighborhood Mob except Barflies keeps away for 15 hours). Success by a wide margin / no hints: they also notice the smell is *the potion itself* leaking, which tells them the Barflies can't smell it — foreshadowing that the Sprawl won't be repelled. ~15 min.

**S1-5 · The Saferoom & Jonai** *(npc)*
*Tag: Mastering • Packed Halls • NPC // Jonai (Gnome SYSTEM ERROR Master, L5, non-combatant)*

Read-aloud: *The saferoom door hisses shut on the howling and for the first time in hours you can hear yourselves breathe. On the bunk in the corner a gnome in a red cap sits with his knees up. He doesn't look up. "I'm not a Bopca," he says. "Before you ask."*

GM: **Want:** to know what happened to Xaggra without having to leave the room. **Friction:** he's ashamed — he stayed inside; he'll deflect into contract-law complaints about Borant. **Tell:** flinches at any buzzing (an insect, a spell hum). **Deal:** find Xaggra — alive or not — and "I'll owe you the only thing I've got, which is knowing how this place works." He knows: the Sprawl is where the buzzing comes from; the Potion of Clarity "undoes what they do"; the Packrooms have no exits "unless you're holding a cat." Terri and Jonai in the same room is a good scene — she's already asked him about the pickaxe. ~20 min.

**Break points, Session 1:**
- *Smaller Goal:* the saferoom, with Terri's map copied and Jonai's ask accepted. (Open the Bronze box from S1-1 here.)
- *Cliffhanger:* if they push on into the Pack Alley Pall — the "We are all dead, and this is Hell" wall and the open grave (S2-0), with the Pack Rat tripping someone in. Roll commercials on the fall.

### Session 2 — "The Pall"  (Mastering; Pack Alley Pall → the Packrooms)

Recap. Clock ~10 hrs.

**S2-0 · The Open Grave** *(combat)*
*Tag: Mastering • Pack Alley Pall • Combat // Weak Pack Rats (1/2) in the grave; then the Barflie-Murderer (Level 5, 5 HB) + Moderate Homogenous Humors (2/3) from the other side*

Read-aloud: *The blue-green fades to bone-grey, the alleys straighten into a grid, and the torches give out; the stones themselves glow like a hospital at night. Sprayed across the wall in something dark: WE ARE ALL DEAD AND THIS IS HELL. The floor in front of it has been dug out. Something white brushes your ankle.*

GM: DEX check or Take Down into the grave; Pack Rats with Advantage on whoever's prone. Next round, from the grave's far end, **the Barflie that used to be a crawler** — red compound eyes, human hands, and an **enchanted pickaxe**. Level 5, one extra Health Bar over a Barflie, 16 to hit, 2d6 damage. Simultaneously Humors from the other direction. This is the session's hard fight — split the party's targets (Humors want to merge; the Barflie wants to Backwash). **The pickaxe is Terri's proof.** If Terri is with them she goes straight for it and says nothing until it's over. ~45 min.

**S2-1 · The Tombs** *(exploration)*
*Tag: Mastering • Pack Alley Pall • Exploration // skill puzzle + one trap, no Mob count*

Read-aloud: *The walls stop being walls. They're tombs — real ones, with real names, dragged here from somewhere with the rest of the collapse. "Beloved grandparent." "Gone fishing." A crack of stone from around the corner: someone is levering a lid.*

GM: Two beats. First, **the other crawler** at the tomb — a rival-party looter (use the Toolkit's quick rule: any check against them is Unopposed, Difficulty 12). Let them talk; the looter knows where the "orange light" is. Second, **the sliver of orange light** where white rats vanish into a crack: a small tomb, a lidless sarcophagus. Skill puzzle, 2 successes (STR to widen the crack, Detect Trap on the sarcophagus — it *is* trapped, GM's choice: darts, Floor Number d6). Inside: a real skeleton, an **enchanted weapon** across its chest (1 Silver benefit), and a **Potion of Clarity**. If this is the party's first Potion, say so out loud: *this is the one.* ~30 min.

**S2-2 · The Fish in the Suit** *(exploration / npc)*
*Tag: Mastering • Pack Alley Pall • Exploration // planted complication #5 — the murdered Kua-Tin*

Read-aloud: *A corpse without a dot. A fish-headed alien in a Borant blazer, one small round hole in the blazer, and the System AI, for once, quiet — then: "I'd, uh, step back from that one, if I were you."*

GM: The Game Guide's promise: identify the killer, earn Borant goodwill (AI Favor). Investigation: the bullet is a service round; there's a scorch pattern that says close range; the admin's clipboard is a **contract** — Jonai's, with Xaggra's name struck through. Resolve per the thread table: (a) the Barflie-murderer's stolen gun is in the grave; or (b) Terri's pistol is missing a round and she goes very still when they mention it. Don't resolve it for them. ~20 min.

**S2-3 · The Packrooms** *(combat / exploration — a clock)*
*Tag: Mastering • Packrooms • Combat // Mirror Cats: 1 to start, +1 every other round up to 2× party size — escape, don't win*

Read-aloud: *The ceiling drops until you're stooping — then opens into an office. Desk, rolling chair, filing cabinet. The door opens onto an office. Desk, chair, cabinet. Your minimap goes grey with a polite chime. There is no sound at all until, from a desk, a meow: a hairless cat, licking its paw — and then it turns, and there's only half of it.*

GM: Every other round another Mirror Cat, until 2× the party. Damage doesn't transfer between a cat's halves; kill either half and both die. Phase Claw → Confused (next Action attacks an ally). **Two exits, both taught by Jonai:** drink a **Potion of Clarity** (the nearest doorway becomes an exit to the Packed Halls) or **grapple a Mirror Cat mid-phase** (takes *one* crawler to the Pack Alley Pall — with an angry cat). A party of two can split: one drinks, one grabs. Each ninth room: the typewriter's page, *"In is out, and out is in… freedom comes from clarity, not without, but from within"* — that's the potion hint, read it once. Nothing they take stays; the furniture comes back. *Manifest Space* achievement when they get out: no box, but each crawler gets a Level. ~40 min.

**Break points, Session 2:**
- *Smaller Goal:* out of the Packrooms with the pickaxe, a Potion of Clarity, and the truth about the fish — Terri says "Then we go to the bar."
- *Cliffhanger:* a party member is still in the Packrooms when the session ends, cat in hand, halfway through a wall.

### Session 3 — "Patti Render's Beer and Tenders"  (Things Blow Up; the Sprawl → Boss)

Recap. Clock ~20 hrs. If they don't have a Potion of Clarity, run complication #6 on the way in: a rival party of three fighting over one.

**S3-0 · Neon and Chromed** *(combat)*
*Tag: Blow Up • The Sprawl • Combat // Moderate Barflies (2/3); trap: CON check or first round is Evades-only at Disadvantage*

Read-aloud: *Colour. After the grey, it hurts: panes of filthy glass in the walls, and behind them, neon — Syndicate Standard letters advertising beer, tenders, "legal services." The signs blink. Faster. Faster. One of you realises you've stopped walking.*

GM: The trap's CON check; failures can only Evade (at Disadvantage) in round 1 while a Moderate group of Barflies drops in. Backwash = **Washed** — say clearly what that means (die Washed and you come back as one of them, Skills and all). The Pack Repellant does nothing here. A Barflie killed with an Amazing hit drops a spool of **cable** (item) and the players notice the nests are *behind the neon* — the doors between the glass. ~35 min.

**S3-1 · Xaggra's Nest** *(combat → npc)*
*Tag: Blow Up • The Sprawl • Combat // Xaggra (Level 3 half-Barflie, 3 HB) + Weak Barflies (1/2), +1 Weak group every round after 3 unless the corpses are dealt with*

Read-aloud: *A bar ripped out of Detroit and left to rot for forty years. Bottles, crates, and on the floor under the pool table, bodies — crawler bodies — and they're moving, slowly, the way a thing moves when it's becoming a different thing. In the corner, a small figure in a red cap with diamonds where his eyes should be turns and buzzes at you.*

GM: Xaggra attacks — he can't help it. He's Level 3; give him Barflie stats with three Health Bars and *no Backwash yet* (he isn't finished). **Saving him:** get a Potion of Clarity into him — a grapple + a free hand, or reduce him to his last bar first and then pour it. The corpses under the table are the clock: after round 3, a Weak group of fresh Barflies stands up *every round* unless someone burns, drags, or clarifies the bodies (a thrown Potion of Clarity over the pile stops it — expensive, heroic). Loot: old high-proof alcohol (a *fire source*, keep that in your pocket for the Boss), minor crawler loot, and **Barflie residue** — alchemy ingredient. Saved Xaggra: he buzzes, then sobs, then says Jonai's name. ~40 min.

**S3-2 · B-B-B-Boss Battle — Court Is in Session** *(combat, boss)*
*Tag: Blow Up • The Sprawl • BOSS // The Bar Render (L8, 11 HB) + 2 Barflies always (she replaces them the round after both die) + Terri if earned*

Use the intersection map on p. 38 for the approach, and the bar on p. 16 (or generate a "Building" map) for the chamber.

Read-aloud (entrance): *"Patti Render's Beer and Tenders — Bar and Grill." The door locks behind you. Two Barflies the size of bouncers peel off the wall. From the taps, a monotone: "Sometimes, legalities are civil. Sometimes, they're criminal. This time? They're a full-on battle royale." A cartoon courtroom unfolds in the air: a nine-foot woman in a dusty suit and spectacles under the word DEFENDANT — and under PLAINTIFFS, your faces.*

Then the AI's announcement — read the box's tone: she's tired, she's furious, she's *good at this*.

**Round-by-round frame:**
- **Round 1:** the bailiffs (2 Barflies) close; Patti stays behind the bar and opens with a **Fiery Cocktail** (30 ft, 10 ft blast). She never leaves the bar. Tell them that.
- **Objections:** any player who *argues* — CHA or Lore vs 14+F, one attempt per crawler per round, in character — makes her *hear it*: she loses her next Cocktail. Legal puns earn Advantage. This is the spotlight for the quiet player.
- **Order a drink:** a crawler who steps to the bar and orders something specific gets served — no attacks against them that round. Once per crawler; she's not stupid.
- **Recess:** kill both bailiffs in the same round and she spends her next round summoning the next pair (no attacks). Her "two Barflies at all times" is a rule she has to obey too.
- **Light the bar:** any fire (her cocktail thrown back, the nest's high-proof alcohol, a spell) within 5 ft of the shelves → the bar burns: 1d6 Fire per round to everything within 10 ft, her included, and she *will not leave it* — it's her retirement. The players' best trick, and it's the *Neighborhood's* trick: turning her unfairness back on her.
- **Terri present:** her Knight's Shield eats one Cocktail a round; she takes Punch hits meant for the party. If they're holding option (b) over her, she fights *harder*.
- **Xaggra/Jonai present:** they don't fight. Jonai narrates the courtroom's rules to the players — "she has to hear an objection, it's her own bit" — which is how you hand the weakness to a table that hasn't found it.
- **Melee warning:** Punch → Held on a Major Fail. Held crawlers eat the next Cocktail. Say it before the first melee attack.
- **Scale on the fly:** rout → a bailiff Backwashes the healer; wipe → the AI "objects" on the party's behalf, once.

When she falls: *"The defense… rests."* Bronze Boss Box + **Neighborhood Map**, +1 Level each. The System AI, if Xaggra is alive: "Huh. Contract error resolved. Congratulations on your new Game Guide, I guess."

**Break points, Session 3:** the Boss. If time runs out at S3-1, the Cliffhanger is the corpses standing up.

## 5. People (NPC cards)

- **Terri Campion, "Terri Cam"** — Crawler #13,123,444, L7, Human, no class. Detroit cop who wanted to be a knight; first box gave her the Knight's Shield. *Want:* the pickaxe killer. *Friction:* trusts nobody's eyes till she's checked them; won't give up her notebook map. *Tell:* "sir/ma'am" as a warning. Gear: Knight's Shield, service pistol (count the rounds), notebook map of the Packed Halls. Survives → a lawful, exhausting ally on Floor 2.
- **Jonai** — Gnome SYSTEM ERROR Master, L5, non-combatant, looks like a Bopca and hates it. *Want:* Xaggra's fate. *Friction:* shame; deflects into contract law. *Tell:* flinches at buzzing. Knows the three secrets of the Neighborhood (Sprawl = buzzing; Clarity undoes Washed; Packrooms need a cat). Reward: Game Guide status if Xaggra is saved.
- **Xaggra** — Gnome SYSTEM ERROR Trainee, L3, half-Barflie. Eyes gone to diamonds; can only buzz. Attacks on sight; Potion of Clarity restores him. Saved → co-Game Guide, and a tearful reunion.
- **The Barflie-Murderer** — a crawler who killed with an enchanted pickaxe, got Washed in the Packrooms, died, and stood up. Level 5, 5 Health Bars of 2, 16 to hit, 2d6. Still carries the pickaxe (and, option (a), a stolen pistol). No name — Terri never learned it, which bothers her.
- **The Bar Render (Patti Render)** — L8 Neighborhood Boss, nine feet, dusty suit, spectacles, a glass in each hand. Thirty years a public defender; the bar is her retirement. Wants order in her court. "The defense… rests."

## 6. Secrets & Clues

1. **One Tracking check per session** keeps you un-lost; otherwise draw your own map. The minimap lies here.
2. **Keep moving** — Canidnas add a member every round you stand still.
3. **Aim the packs** — Canidnas and Pack Rats will fight each other over a corpse (Animal Handling).
4. **Eyeballs merge** — keep Humors apart; ranged and blunt do nothing to them.
5. **Clarity is the key** — cures Washed, saves Xaggra, resets the map trap, exits the Packrooms. Three in the Neighborhood.
6. **The Packrooms have no doors** — unless you're drinking Clarity or holding a cat.
7. **The pickaxe** — Terri's killer is the Barflie in the grave. The pickaxe is the proof.
8. **The fish in the suit** was shot at close range with a service round. Count Terri's bullets.
9. **She has to hear an objection** — it's her own bit. And she will never leave the bar, even when it's burning.

- **Terri Campion** as an ally at the Boss and beyond.

## 8. Cuts

**Two nights (~3.5 hrs each):** Session 1 = S1-0, S1-1, S1-3 (Terri), S1-5 (Jonai), then S2-0 (the grave — the pickaxe fight) as the climax; break on *"Then we go to the bar."* Session 2 = S2-1 (tomb, for the Potion), S3-0, S3-1, S3-2. Drop the Packrooms and the Kua-Tin; Jonai mentions both as rumors.

**One night (~3.5–4 hrs):** S1-0 → S1-1 → S1-3 (Terri gives them the Potion of Clarity from her own kit — she's been saving it) → S3-0 → S3-1 (Xaggra) → S3-2. Five fights is a lot; make S1-1 the pit-them scene with no player combat, and S3-0 a trap-and-run instead of a fight. Jonai appears only as the voice on the saferoom intercom.

## Optional Texture — the Naturalist

The summary mentions a "Pack Alley Naturalist" who hides in the Neighborhood, filming the Mobs for an unpublishable documentary — and then he never appears again. Use him as a **voice**: a camera drone the size of a fist that follows the party at a distance and whispers David Attenborough narration about *them* ("The crawlers, exhausted, make the classic mistake…"). He never fights. Killing the drone earns a Bronze box and a very annoyed showrunner. Ignore him entirely if your table won't find it funny.
