# Food is Love — Worked Adventure (Quest, Floor 1 or 2; 1–3 sessions of 3–4 hrs)

*Toolkit reference: Quest: Food is Love, pp. 61–69. Theme: Pizza Rat, Now a Restaurant. Table: 2–3 players. Three sessions (one per Quest Stage, ~3.5 hrs each) with cuts at the end.*

This is a Quest, not a Neighborhood, so the template bends: **Quest Stages replace quarters, and each Stage is a session and a break point.** The book is explicit that the Stages are story beats, not locations — so this adventure moves; you drop it *on top of* whatever Neighborhood the party is in (the Serving Warrens is the natural home, since the Rat-kin are already there, but any floor-1 or floor-2 Neighborhood's Mobs are the enemies). Player confusion at the start is intended: nobody explains what a Quest is on the tutorial floors.

It's a *Romeo and Juliet* in reverse — two families who *want* the marriage and two kids who'd rather die than go through with it — and the trick of running it is that almost everyone in it is a **non-combatant with a white dot**. The fights are against the Neighborhood; the Rat-kin are the people the players have to decide whether to love.

---

## 1. The One-Paragraph Answers

**What's going on.** A Rat-kin community that raised itself on *The Godfather* and *The Sopranos* runs pizza parlours and wants off this floor. Their plan: breed **Rat Hooligans** (Brute × Shaman children, stronger and smarter than either) by arranging marriages between the two sides of the family — the meat-eating Brutes under **Godpapa John**, the mushroom-eating Shamans under **Godmama Mia**. This season's match, **Snookie** (a Brute who makes paninis that heal) and **Luigi** (a Shaman who cooks pasta that makes you forget), have run away — *separately*, because they can't stand each other. The first thing the players notice: **a Rat Brute fighting on their side**, or **a Rat Shaman saving their lives** — a white-dotted Mob, talking, which the AI will point out is a thing that doesn't happen until Floor 3.

**Who's the Boss and what does it want.** The **Critical Consensus** — a Shambling Berserker stitched together from twenty-six dead food influencers, all arms and phones and heads talking about restaurants — wants **to eat everything it sees and post about it.** The producers built it to punish whoever spoils the story. **Weaknesses, as player actions (the book gives all of these — the Quest Items are the clue trail):** *If they oversalt food and let it eat it* — "Idiots love salt. Just give him a heart attack" (Snookie's diary) — it **loses** a Health Bar instead of healing. *If they snap dry pasta* — "Never snap dry pasta" (Luigi's recipe) — it turns on the snapper: a *taunt* that pulls it off anyone else. *If they say something offensive about food* — "Pineapple is the best topping," "we aren't comping your meal" (the 1-star review's influencer tropes) — same effect, but it ignores repeats. *If it grabs someone*, it **stops for a round to take a picture**, defenses halved, and the others can free the victim. *If they keep the lights on* — torches, the gas stoves — it's slow and clumsy; **in the dark its damage is ×4**. And *if anyone is carrying food in their Inventory*, it moves twice as fast toward them — which is a weakness you can weaponize: the crawler with the biggest bag of oversalted bread is the tank.

**How the Mobs interact.** The Rat-kin are allies with white dots (Brutes: noise/light/insult → Roid Rage, one more swing at 0%; Shamans: fire, stay at range, disengage in melee; Hooligans: ambush, heal, expendable once the marriages happen). Neighborhood Mobs raid the Rat-kin for ingredients (Goblins want pineapples for weapons, Bad Llamas want sauce for their throats, Kobolds want basil). **Chef BoyardOozes** are kitchen accidents — tomato-sauce slimes; *cold* or *heat* does ×2 and *stops their regen*; they leave slick trails. **Levers:** a walk-in freezer; a lit stove; an insult aimed at a Brute you want *to* rage (against a Neighborhood Mob).

**Clock budget.** Quests don't wait for the collapse the way Neighborhoods do — the book tells you to *accelerate* Stages if the party dawdles. Budget **30 hrs across three sessions**, but the real clock is **the marriage**: if the players take too long, the families find the kids first (Push, ~22 hrs), and the wedding happens without them — and then the Boss spawns in the kitchen anyway (Hard stop, ~30). Pre-picked complications: **#2** (Goblins intercepting a pizza delivery — the party's first chance to see Rat-kin as people); **#4** (a Shaman's poker game with Goblins — join or fight); **#1** (a Hooligan offering to "distract the Mobs" for a price) as a Stage 2 seed.

## 2. The Family (decide before Stage 1)

| Who | Faction | Wants | The tell |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Godpapa John** (Brute L8, non-combatant) | Meat / Brutes | Snookie home, married, making Hooligans | Serves a pizza buffet while visibly panicking; calls everyone "honored guests" |
| **Godmama Mia** (Shaman L10, non-combatant) | Fungi / Shamans | Luigi home, married | Hums between sentences; offers fungal pizza samples (which may cause permanent brain damage) |
| **Snookie** (Brute L7, non-combatant) | Meat | To have ratlings with *loving* parents, not two rats who hate each other; a good panini; her toaster | Chops corpses into sandwich meat; her food heals; she's angry all the time |
| **Luigi** (Shaman L8, non-combatant) | Fungi | Real air, real soil, out of the dungeon; not a bunch of rugrats | Draws enemy attention on purpose; carries fresh pizza ingredients as bait; his pasta makes you forget |
| **Cousin Vinnie D'Parmigiano** (Hooligan L50, guildmaster, "the Rules Lawyer") | Neither | Some happiness for the kids; the system bent, not broken | Roams freely; auto-teleports home if attacked; a divorce lawyer |
| **Alfredo** (Bopca, non-combatant) | The saferoom | To not cook. Ever. | Has let Luigi run his kitchen; pretends not to know where the kids are |
| **Squeak 42** (Crawler #32,618, L9, Brown Rat, 4 PK skulls, Tommy gun) | Wild card | GM's call | The first crawler Luigi ever served |

**The three endings** (the book's, and they're all playable):
- **Matchmaker** — the kids agree to marry: Silver Ring Box + the Rat-kin's friendship for the floor + Family Reunion Box (Gold).
- **Troublemaker** — the kids run away together: Gold Stain Remover Box + Family Reunion Box (Silver); the families are hurt but the kids are alive.
- **Star-Crossed Lovers** — the party tries to capture them, they flee the saferoom, fight to the death; **the Quest fails**; Gold Asshole Box; the families become enemies forever.

Whatever they choose, **the Boss comes after.** Where it spawns depends on who the producers are punishing.

## 3. Scene Ladder (Stages as Sessions)

### Stage 1 — "If You're Here, You're Family"  (Session 1)

**Q1-0 · Cold Open — A Rat on Your Side** *(combat → npc)*
*Tag: Stage 1 • Strong Start • Combat // a Moderate group of Neighborhood Mobs (2/3) vs the party — and a Rat Brute who switches sides when they're winning*

Read-aloud: *You're already in it — [Neighborhood Mobs] on three sides, and a rat the size of a fridge fighting beside them. Then one of you lands a hit that matters, and the rat looks at the Mobs, looks at you, and hits the nearest Mob with a crossbow butt. "Yeah," he says, in Jersey. "I'm gonna need a favor."*

GM: Pick the Neighborhood's Mobs. The Brute (call him **Larry**) fights *with* them from the moment they gain the upper hand; his ask afterwards is the Quest hook — "come meet the family." The AI fires the achievement: *An Offer You Shouldn't Refuse* → Bronze Adventurer Box, plus the AI's deeply confused explanation that this isn't a Quest, it *can't* be a Quest, and pay attention anyway. **Alternate start** (*rattus ex machina*): a Rat Shaman saves them from a wipe instead, and they owe *her* side first. Which family they meet first is decided here. ~35 min.

**Q1-1 · The Family Dinner** *(npc)*
*Tag: Stage 1 • Godpapa John or Godmama Mia • NPC // the buffet; the ask*

Read-aloud (Godpapa): *A pizza parlour with checkered tablecloths and a photo of a rat in a tuxedo on every wall. A mountain of a rat in a tailored suit rises from the head of the table. "Honored guests. Sit. Eat. We got the meat-lover's, we got the meat-lover's supreme—" His paw is shaking on the pizza cutter.*

Read-aloud (Godmama): *Incense, psychedelic silk, a mural of a mushroom with a halo. An elderly rat in seven colours hums three notes and takes your hands. "You're hungry. Mm-hm. Try this. No — try this."*

GM: Rapport first: the Godparent answers questions **eagerly and honestly even when they're wrong** — that's the comedy and the humanity. Then the ask: *two of our young ran away — Snookie (Brutes) and Luigi (Shamans). Bring them home. Any means necessary.* The two sides don't feud, but play up the cultural sniping ("mushroom people") so the twist lands later. **They do not say** the kids were meant to marry each other. ~25 min.

**Q1-2 · Dinner Is Interrupted** *(combat)*
*Tag: Stage 1 • the parlour • Combat // at Godpapa's: a Chef BoyardOoze (1 for 2p, 1 + a slick-trail second at 3p); at Godmama's: a Strong raid of Neighborhood Mobs (3/5) — the Rat-kin take the hits*

Read-aloud (Godpapa's): *A scream from the kitchen — not pain, joy — and a wave of tomato sauce comes through the swinging doors with an empty can riding on top of it like a crown. It reaches for the nearest cook with a tendril of cheese, and the cook reaches back.*

Read-aloud (Godmama's): *The door comes off its hinges. [Goblins with pineapples / Llamas with glowing throats / Kobolds shouting about basil] pour in and go straight for the pantry. Godmama Mia stops humming.*

GM: **This is supposed to be very dangerous**, and the Rat-kin are supposed to *die for the players* to prove the alliance is real. Play it: a Brute steps in front of a crawler and takes a fireball; a Shaman burns her Mana to save the healer and goes down. If it goes badly, **Godpapa or Godmama dies**, dying words about the child. Chef BoyardOoze notes: slick trails (+2 Difficulty on DEX), Tendril → Held + Saucy (acid per round), **regenerates a bar every round** — drag it into the **walk-in freezer** (cold stops regen, ×2) or onto a **lit stove**. Kitchen accident texture (Quest Event #1): a cook who lost a hand to the grinder throws himself in "to feed the family one last time." ~45 min.

**Q1-3 · The Other Family** *(npc — the twist)*
*Tag: Stage 1 • the other parlour • NPC // relief, trust, and the reveal*

Read-aloud: *The other Godparent has heard about the fight before you arrive. "You bled for them. Then you'll bleed for us." A long pause. "The children were to be married. Snookie to Luigi. It was going to be — it was going to be beautiful."*

GM: The twist: **arranged marriage; both sides wanted it; both kids ran.** The players now know what "bring them home" means. Gossip (Quest Event #2) from a boy and girl Rat-kin: the boy sneers that *he'd* do his duty; the girl retorts that he isn't the one expected to "pump out ten babies a month." Let the players feel the first pull toward the kids. ~20 min.

**Break points, Stage 1:**
- *Smaller Goal:* both families met, the alliance sealed in blood, the marriage revealed.
- *Cliffhanger:* a Godparent dying in the raid, and the other family's door opening.

### Stage 2 — "A Recipe for Disaster"  (Session 2)

Recap. Clock ~10 hrs.

**Q2-0 · The Clue Trail** *(exploration)*
*Tag: Stage 2 • the Neighborhood • Exploration // four Quest Items, in any order*

GM: Spread the items across the Neighborhood's quarters — one per scene, with a Neighborhood encounter between each (use the Neighborhood's own cards at Weak power; this session is about *finding*, not fighting):
- **The 1-star review** taped to a saferoom door: Alfredo's cooking is a war crime; *directions to Alfredo's saferoom* ("much better cuisine, actually made by Luigi") — and a page of influencer phrases that are **the taunts for the Boss**. Read-aloud: *"Zero stars. The Bopca's 'risotto' should be reported to the Health and Safety tier. Went two doors down. Whoever's cooking there — marry me."*
- **Snookie's diary page** (from Godpapa, or near where she slept): Cousin Vinnie's advice on *initiating a divorce* — she's already planning the end of a marriage that hasn't happened. In the margin: **"Idiots love salt. Just give him a heart attack."**
- **The destroyed toaster** — a Kobold's Danger Dingo ate it and passed it; Snookie's name carved on it; patchwork repairs; a small crawler tragedy. Whoever returns it to her has her trust.
- **The recipe for pasta aglio e olio** on a saferoom napkin in a Shaman's *legible* handwriting — Luigi's. **"Never snap dry pasta."**
~40 min across the session.

**Q2-1 · The Hooligan Ambush** *(combat)*
*Tag: Stage 2 • anywhere • Combat // 1 Rat Hooligan (2p) / 2 (3p) + 1 Brute + 1 Shaman — the Quest's hardest fight*

Read-aloud: *A rat leaning on a pipe, filing his claws. Lean, quiet, a longsword he hasn't drawn. "You're looking for the kids." Not a question. "Word of advice: don't." The pipe he's leaning on is warm. Behind you, someone coughs.*

GM: The Hooligans are the *product* — the previous generation's Hooligan children — and they want Snookie and Luigi to stay unmarried **so they stay irreplaceable**. Rat Hooligan: L8, **8 Health Bars**, longsword 2d8, Firebolt 2d6, *Heal Others*. At two players, one Hooligan is Strong on its own; the Brute and Shaman are there for texture, and the Hooligan heals them. Kill the healer first; say it. **Dialogue mid-fight** makes the motive clear: "You think the *families* want Hooligans? They want *replacements*." Nonlethal capture (Held, charm, necromancy on the Brute) → interrogation: the kids **fled separately**, hate each other, cooked for NPCs on the way (Snookie's paninis heal; Luigi's pasta erases memory). Snookie went to a **Tutorial Guild** first — Vinnie's. ~45 min.

**Q2-2 · Cousin Vinnie** *(npc)*
*Tag: Stage 2 • Vinnie's guildhall • NPC // the Rules Lawyer, Hooligan L50, non-combatant*

Read-aloud: *A guildhall with a law degree on the wall and a rat behind the desk in a suit that cost more than your house. "Sit. You're here about the kids. Everybody's here about the kids. Let me save you time: they hate each other because they can't see past their families. And they want the same thing." He spreads his paws. "To live a life that isn't defined by their parents. Also I can't help you. Legally."*

GM: **Want:** some happiness for the kids; the system bent. **Friction:** he won't *act* — his contract; he auto-teleports home if attacked; he only bends rules for people he likes. **Tell:** every sentence has a clause. He confirms the saferoom (Alfredo's) and gives the players the frame they'll need in Stage 3: *both kids want out; neither is the stereotype.* He'd rather they marry than die — "some happiness now is better than nothing." If the party likes him, he can become their **Game Guide**. ~20 min.

**Q2-3 · Squeak 42 (optional)** *(npc / combat)*
*Tag: Stage 2 • anywhere • NPC // Crawler #32,618, L9 brown rat, Tommy gun, 4 PK skulls*

GM: A real rat with a crawler number and unlimited ammo, the first crawler Luigi ever fed. GM's decision whether he talks, whether he's someone's pet, whether he's hunting Luigi or protecting him. A useful complication if the party is tearing through Stage 2 too fast: he's *between them and the saferoom*, and four Player-Killer Skulls means he's already killed crawlers. ~15 min if used.

**Break points, Stage 2:**
- *Smaller Goal:* all four Quest Items, Vinnie's blessing, the saferoom's location.
- *Cliffhanger:* the Hooligan ambush, mid-fight, as the Hooligan says "don't."

### Stage 3 — "Better Ingredients, Better Berserker"  (Session 3)

Recap. Clock ~20 hrs.

**Q3-0 · Alfredo's Saferoom** *(npc — the argument)*
*Tag: Stage 3 • the saferoom • NPC // Snookie, Luigi, Alfredo; the truce*

Read-aloud: *A saferoom like every other, except the smell — garlic, olive oil, something browning — and the yelling. "—being pregnant is bad enough without living off mushrooms and SLIME—" "—must be nice to be too dumb to know when your meat's expired—" A Bopca in an apron sees you and says, flatly, "There is no one here. Would you like some risotto."*

GM: Use the map on p. 60 (the restaurant with the kitchen behind the counter, the walk-in, the dining room, the patio). The kids argue in stereotypes. **The truce** is a player-driven social scene — resolve it the book's way: point out that *neither of them fits the stereotype* (she cooks; he's ambitious; Vinnie said so), or **order food** — force Luigi to help Alfredo cook, and watch Snookie be surprised that he makes real food; order a sandwich and watch Luigi be surprised that she makes a panini worth eating. Give each player one lever: Snookie's toaster (returned → trust), Luigi's recipe (quoted → he listens), the diary (Snookie finds out the party read it — angry, then honest). **Outcomes:**
- Kids reconcile → the party advises **marry** (*Matchmaker*) or **run** (*Troublemaker*). Either way they refuse to abandon Alfredo when the Boss comes.
- The party tries to **capture** them → they flee the saferoom (the AI reminds everyone that combat in a saferoom is punished), set an ambush outside, fight to the death; **Quest fails**, *Star-Crossed Lovers*.
~40 min.

**Q3-1 · The Announcement** *(transition)*

Read-aloud: *"Attention crawlers! We have a situation in this saferoom. It seems our resident Bopca decided that basic culinary competence is too much to ask, and invited an unregistered NPC to cook. Tier 3 Health and Safety Violation. Effective immediately, this chamber is downgraded from 'Saferoom' to 'Free-for-All Buffet.' Weapon locks? Disengaged. Finish your soup quickly. Or don't."*

GM: **Where the Boss spawns** is the producers' punishment: if the players *defied* the production (advised the kids to run, or to marry on their own terms), the Critical Consensus spawns **outside**, the door locks, and the party has time to plan and light the room; if the kids **die or agree to marry**, it spawns **inside the kitchen**, knocks over the water pots, shorts the power — **lights out**, and the fight starts in the dark at ×4. Alfredo is the target either way: frozen in fear, non-combatant, and the AI won't let him be harmed — but the Boss will *try*.

**Q3-2 · B-B-B-Boss Battle — The Critical Consensus** *(combat, boss)*
*Tag: Stage 3 • the saferoom kitchen • BOSS // Critical Consensus (L8, Huge, 11 HB of 5); ×4 in the dark; Luigi and Snookie as non-combatant support*

Read-aloud (entrance): *A dinner bell. Classical music. Faces slide up the wall like a social feed — yours, and a thing that is mostly arms, every arm holding a phone or a fork, six heads talking over each other about a tasting menu. The most recent comment under your face reads: "overrated." Then the lights go out.*

Then the AI box (paraphrase: twenty-six failed food influencers, four times more dangerous in the dark, give us your best duck face, like and subscribe).

**Round-by-round frame:**
- **Light first.** In the dark it hits ×4. Torches; **the gas stoves** — a crawler at the range with a spark is the most important person in the room; Luigi's Firebolt can light them from across the kitchen. Say what ×4 means out loud so nobody learns it by dying.
- **Constant Hunger:** it eats any food it sees, heals 1d4 bars, and moves **twice as fast** toward anyone carrying food. Everyone with food in Inventory is bait — which means *the party chooses who it charges*.
- **Over-Seasoned:** the kitchen has salt. A crawler who spends an Action oversalting a dish (or Snookie, who's been planning this) and leaves it in its path → it eats, and **loses a bar**. Do this every round you can. That's the win condition the diary handed them.
- **Snap dry pasta** → it turns on the snapper for the round (the taunt). **Say something offensive about food** → same, once per phrase; "pineapple is the best topping," "we aren't comping your meal," "do you know who I am" back at it. The 1-star review is the phrasebook.
- **Foodporn:** the first time it Holds each crawler, it **stops for a round to take a picture** — defenses halved, and the others can pull the victim free. A grabbed crawler is an opportunity, not a loss.
- **Slam:** 2d8+4 Necrotic, Held; Major Fail adds Staggered. In the dark that's 2d8+16.
- **Luigi and Snookie** don't fight — they *cook*. Luigi suggests options and taunts ("your risotto is a war crime"); Snookie salts. Alfredo does nothing; keep him alive.
- **Scale on the fly:** rout → the lights go out again (a phone-arm smashes a torch); wipe → it stops to post a review of the fight — one free round.

**After:** Bronze Boss Box; **Family Reunion Box** — **Gold** if the kids agreed to marry, **Silver** if they ran away together; nothing if the Quest failed. Plus the ending's achievement box. And the Rat-kin's eternal friendship "for all the time you have left until Level Collapse" — which, on this floor, is not nothing.

**Break points, Stage 3:** the Boss. If time runs out at Q3-0, end on the announcement — the lights going out is the cliffhanger.

## 4. Power Levels Used

| Card | 2 players | 3 players |
|---|---|---|
| Q1-0 Cold Open | 2 Neighborhood Mobs + Larry | 3 + Larry |
| Q1-2 Interruption | 1 BoyardOoze / Strong raid (3) | 1 BoyardOoze + a second on the trail / Strong raid (5) |
| Q2-0 between clues | Weak Neighborhood Mobs (1) ×2–3 | Weak (2) ×2–3 |
| Q2-1 Hooligans | 1 Hooligan + Brute + Shaman | 2 Hooligans + Brute + Shaman |
| Q3-2 Boss | Critical Consensus | Critical Consensus (spawn outside so they can plan) |

**The Hooligan** (8 HB, heals) is the deadliest thing in the Quest for a small table — deadlier than the Boss if the Boss is in the light. Two players: one Hooligan, and let the Brute be the one it heals so the players can see the mechanic.

## 5. People (NPC cards)

## 6. Secrets & Clues

1. **"Bring them home" means "make them marry."** Both families wanted it; both kids ran, separately.
2. **They hate each other because of their families**, not each other. Neither fits the stereotype (Vinnie).
3. **"Idiots love salt."** Oversalted food damages the Boss instead of healing it.
4. **"Never snap dry pasta."** Snapping it pulls the Boss onto you.
5. **The 1-star review** is a phrasebook of things that offend it — and directions to Alfredo's.
6. **It's ×4 in the dark.** Torches; the gas stoves.
7. **It stops to take a picture** when it grabs someone. Pull them free then.
8. **Food in your bag makes you the target.** Choose who.
9. **The Hooligans want the kids unmarried** — so they stay irreplaceable.
10. **Cold or heat** stops a BoyardOoze's regeneration. The walk-in; the stove.

## 7. Rewards

- **An Offer You Shouldn't Refuse** → Bronze Adventurer Box (met a Mob without killing it).
- **Matchmaker** → Silver Ring Box + the Rat-kin's eternal friendship; **Family Reunion Box (Gold)**.
- **Troublemaker** → Gold Stain Remover Box; **Family Reunion Box (Silver)**.
- **Star-Crossed Lovers** → Gold Asshole Box; Quest failed; families hostile.
- **Bronze Boss Box** — the Critical Consensus.
- **Snookie's paninis** (heal) and **Luigi's pasta** (forget) — as long as they're alive and friendly.
- **Cousin Vinnie as Game Guide** — if they like him and he likes them.
- **+1 Level** for Quest completion (more if multi-floor, GM's call).

## 8. Cuts

**Two nights:** S1 = Stage 1 complete (Q1-0 → Q1-3) + the first two Quest Items (review, diary) found on the way to the other family. S2 = the Hooligan ambush (with the toaster and the recipe on the Hooligans — they took them from the kids), Vinnie in one scene, then Stage 3 and the Boss.

**One night:** Q1-0 (Larry) → Q1-1 (one Godparent, who *does* tell them about the marriage) → the Hooligan ambush (all four items on the bodies) → Q3-0 the argument → Q3-2 the Boss. Skip Vinnie; Alfredo delivers his line about the kids wanting the same thing.

## Optional Texture — Alterations

The book invites you to reskin the whole thing: Yakuza rats making sushi (the Boss becomes a stitched-together food critic who reviews omakase), or Klingons running blood-wine pubs. If your Serving Warrens Rat-kin were spared the Rat Bastard's death, the *same* clan shows up here "picked up for a second season" — Godpapa John can be the Rat Bastard's uncle, and the players' choice there is why the family trusts them here.
