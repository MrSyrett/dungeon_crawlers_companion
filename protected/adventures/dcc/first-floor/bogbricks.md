# Bogbricks — Worked Adventure (Floor 1, two sessions)

*Toolkit reference: Neighborhood: Bogbricks, pp. 17–28. Theme: Smooth Jazz and Choppy Waters. Table: 2–3 players, ~2.5 hrs/session.*

This is the run-sheet template filled in. Book stats are referenced by page, not reproduced; the connective tissue, scene order, GM notes, and read-alouds are original.

---

## 1. The One-Paragraph Answers

**What's going on.** Bogbricks is a drowned brick city playing itself a funeral march. The whole Neighborhood has a heartbeat — the Hide-Hitter Crib Daddy's drum kit — and the tempo is the players' compass: faster and louder the closer they get to his jazz hall in Cypress Halls. Two quarters are dry and urban (Gaslamp District, Cypress Halls), two are flooded and overgrown (Still Waters, Vibrant Walls). Everything smells like great food and rotten water, and the first thing players notice is that **the lights and the drums flicker in time**.

**Who's the Boss and what does he want.** The Crib Daddy wants what every bandleader wants: the beat to never stop and the room to keep watching him. His Trash Princesses are his adoring audience and his bodyguards. **The weakness, written as player actions:** *If they distract him* (the achievement *Distracted Daddy* is the book telling you this is the intended solution), the Princesses lose their frenzy bonus and he stops playing — and **he cannot use any of his three drum attacks without drumsticks**. *If they take or wreck his sticks* he's reduced to a club or spear. *If they bring Mad Driscoll*, his musk pulls the Princesses off the stage entirely. And *if they fight him up close*, his stench gives them Sepsis — so the fight rewards ranged attacks, spells, and thrown things, which is exactly what a two-person party needs to hear before round one.

**How the Mobs interact.** Scat Thugs (Gaslamp) are food-motivated traders and trap-nerds who will *talk* if fed or impressed; they answer socially to Mad Driscoll, their folk hero, but the Crib Daddy is the boss of the Neighborhood. Riff Roughers (Cypress Halls) are the Boss's muscle and eat anything, including Scat Thugs. Gobblin' Gators (Still Waters) and Gnawtria (Vibrant Walls) are territorial animals — the Gnawtria tunnels bypass the water entirely. Rayzers are everywhere, above and below, and are the Neighborhood's ambush tax. **Levers:** Ghost eats any Mob that goes near the water (complication 1); a Riff Rougher bar (complication 6) can be pointed at another crawler party; the tunnels let the party skip Still Waters if they've befriended nothing.

**Clock budget.** Floor 1: 5 days = 150 dungeon hours. Bogbricks budget **24 hours**. Comfortable at end of Session 1: **10 hours** spent. Push at **18**: Riff Roughers start hunting the party through the halls. Hard stop at **26**: the drums stop mid-beat — the Crib Daddy has come out to find them (the book says Bosses can't leave their chambers; so instead the Princesses arrive as heralds and *drag* the party toward the jazz hall). Grinding complications to **pre-pick**: #4 (Driscoll bursts through a wall chased by Selina) if the players haven't met either NPC by mid-Session 1; #2 (drums speed up, someone stumbles into the water) as the Session 1 cliffhanger. Clock drainers to keep in the pocket: **Shadowfall** (gas + darkness — costs them a rest) and Table 2 #17 (lost: −1d4×10% of remaining time).

## 2. The Three-Way Fork (decide this before Session 1)

The Neighborhood's real story is a triangle, and the party will land somewhere in it:

| Side with… | They get | They lose | Boss-fight effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mad Driscoll** (help kill Ghost) | Enchanted longsword (two Silver bonuses), 1 Rank Longsword after 2 hrs of training; Scat Thugs stop attacking; **Scat Thugs in the Boss chamber turn on the Crib Daddy** | Selina becomes an enemy; *Reality Sets In* if Driscoll later dies at their hands is off the table | Driscoll's musk distracts the Princesses; Crib Daddy is easier to hit |
| **Selina** (rescue Ghost, kill Driscoll) | Potions of Healing; Selina **and Ghost** fight beside them at the Boss; *Reality Sets In* achievement → Gold Mythbuster Box | Scat Thugs hostile for the rest of the Neighborhood; Driscoll's sword | A Level 7 alligator on their side; Scat Thugs in the chamber fight *against* them |
| **Ghost himself** (meat, Charisma, an Enhanced Pet Biscuit, or the Potion of Reptile Repose) | A gator that follows them; *Where the Wild Things Are* | Driscoll tries to kill them; Selina confronts them | As above but without Selina's potions |
| **Nobody** | Clean hands | Every ally | The straight fight: Crib Daddy + Princesses + Scat Thugs |

Don't railroad the choice — just make sure both Driscoll and Selina *ask* before the end of Session 1.

## 3. Scene Ladder

### Session 1 — "Follow the Drums"  (Discovery → early Mastering; Gaslamp District → Still Waters)

**S1-0 · Cold Open — The Lights Go Out** *(exploration → combat)*
*Tag: Discovery • Gaslamp • Combat // Weak: 1 Scat Thug (2p) / 2 (3p) + Bricktop Barrage*

Read-aloud: *The lamps gutter in time with a bass drum somewhere ahead, then die. In the black: the patter of dozens of small feet, a tug at your belt, a giggle. When the gas catches again, thirty feet up on an iron balcony a raccoon-headed thing in a torn waistcoat holds your [most-loved small item] over its head and grins.*

GM: Pick the item live — a potion, the ranged weapon, a pet carrier. The thief is on a balcony (3 rounds to climb the pillars, 1 to slide down; **the pillars are the Romeo Render trap** — spikes pop when hands grip; 1d6 per round on the pillars). The smart route is the stairs at the alley end, which walk straight under a **Bricktop Barrage** tripwire (Unopposed Detect Trap, "Something's Off"). Fight or negotiate: the thief will trade the item back for food. If the party *disarms the tripwire with style*, the Scat Thugs applaud and this becomes an npc scene (see S1-2). Beat this card in ~30 min.

**S1-1 · The Gaslamp District** *(exploration)*
*Tag: Discovery • Gaslamp • Exploration // no fight — plant clues*

Read-aloud: *Pastel plaster peels off red brick a hundred years old. Wide avenues, alleys every few hundred feet, and above everything the wrought-iron balconies, thick with dying patio plants and small watching eyes. Under the smell of roasting meat: swamp. The drums are steady here, almost lazy.*

GM: Three things to find, in any order — (a) **the shopfront where Selina is buying meat** (S1-2); (b) a **saferoom** door; the Bopca warns them "don't stand still in the water, and don't dance"; (c) the **tempo clue**: a player with Perception/Lore notices the drums speed up when they walk *north* (toward Cypress Halls) and slow when they walk south toward the water. Use Neighborhood Mood #3 (glowing eyes above) as the room's texture. ~15 min.

**S1-2 · Selina & the Meat Stall** *(npc)*
*Tag: Discovery • Gaslamp • NPC // Selina Poe (Crawler #9,329,440, L5)*

Read-aloud: *A woman in waders and a lab-jacket is arguing with a raccoon over a haunch of something. She has a magic spear on her back and a net at her belt, and she keeps glancing at the water. She waves you over like you're late.*

GM: **Want:** find Ghost before Driscoll does. **Friction:** she won't say Ghost is an alligator until they've agreed to help — she's been laughed at. **Tell:** hums along to the drums without noticing. **Deal:** three Potions of Healing now for meat or an escort to Still Waters; the Enchanted Pet Carrier if they help her *catch* him. If they refuse, she goes alone — and shows up later on Driscoll's side of a fight, or dead. This is where the fork opens. ~15 min.

**Fork:** they can go **south to Still Waters** (Selina, Ghost, Driscoll's hunt — the main line) or **west into Vibrant Walls** (S2-1; tunnels). The drums are pulling north, but north is the Boss, and the Bopca said so. Let them choose; the default is south.

**S1-3 · Still Waters — Something Brushes Your Leg** *(combat)*
*Tag: Mastering • Still Waters • Combat // Moderate: 2 Gobblin' Gators (2p) / 3 (3p); Rayzers overhead as terrain*

Read-aloud: *The brick ends and the floor is gone — brackish water to the knee, then the thigh, drifting pallets and a rocking-chair. Vines lace the walls. The drums are far away now, so you can hear the other sound: a slow, heavy slide of something entering the water behind you.*

GM: Run it as the book's Mood #2 — whoever stands perfectly still is passed over; whoever moves gets bitten. Terrain: a floating pallet holds two people; Gators move twice as fast in water; Rayzers on the ceiling fire magic missiles at anyone who climbs onto debris (don't add them to the Mob count — they're the reason not to leave the water). *Bog Bait* triggers here if someone just stands in the water: read it, hand them the Silver Shoe Box. **Then Driscoll's bomb** — mid-fight or right after, an explosion in the water, a dead gator floats up, and a one-legged raccoon on a raft swears at them. ~40 min.

**S1-4 · Mad Driscoll on the Raft** *(npc)*
*Tag: Mastering • Still Waters • NPC // Mad Driscoll (Scat Hero, L6 NPC)*

Read-aloud: *He's three feet of scar tissue and attitude, a peg where a leg should be, a longsword longer than he is, and a satchel that clinks. "You're not the gator," he says, disappointed. "But you'll do. He's here. I can smell him. Help me kill the monster and I'll make you a swordsman."*

GM: **Want:** revenge on Ghost. **Friction:** he won't wait — he'll throw the next bomb whether or not the party is in the blast (1d6 to anyone who doesn't move). **Tell:** taps his peg-leg in time with the drums, hates that he does it. **Deal:** the longsword and a Rank in Longsword (2 hours — costs clock) + the Scat Thugs stand down + "my boys in the jazz hall will *turn* when they see me." If Selina is with the party, this is a standoff: she and Driscoll both look at the players. **This is the session's decision.**

**Break points for Session 1:**
- *Smaller Goal:* they've picked a side and reached the Still Waters saferoom (Bopca: Gordo, surly, will dry their boots). Also: they've marked the **green wood that doesn't drift** — the buried chest (see Clues).
- *Cliffhanger:* run complication #2 — the drums suddenly double-time, someone's foot finds a bedded-down Rayzer, they go under, and **Ghost surfaces** nose-to-nose with them. Roll commercials.

### Session 2 — "Downbeat"  (Mastering → Things Blow Up; Vibrant Walls → Cypress Halls → Boss)

Recap in the AI's voice ("Previously on…"), then note the clock: ~10 hours gone.

**S2-1 · Vibrant Walls — The Squeal in the Path** *(combat)*   ← *the cuttable quarter*
*Tag: Mastering • Vibrant Walls • Combat // Moderate: 2 Gnawtria (2p) / 3 (3p); then Weak: 1–2 Rayzers roused from the riverbed*

Read-aloud: *Dirt and moss underfoot, then flowers — walls of them, hip-high, in colours that don't exist on Earth, with a canopy so far above it might as well be sky. The path splits, and splits again. Half of it is underwater. Something squeals, delighted, and comes through the flowers at ankle height.*

GM: Gnawtria go for the face (knockdown, Armor-Piercing bite, Poison). The scuffle wakes Rayzers in the riverbed — second wave, ranged. **The prize** is what's left after: the Gnawtria hole. Investigation finds it widens into a tunnel network that spirals under the quarter and surfaces near saferooms — **a dry shortcut to Cypress Halls** that skips Still Waters. If the party sided with Selina, this is also where the singing glade is: she's here tossing meat to the water and Ghost comes up to take it. **If you only have one night: cut this card entirely**, and let a Scat Thug mention the tunnels as a rumor. ~40 min.

**S2-2 · The Buried Chest (optional, Still Waters)** *(exploration)*
*Tag: Mastering • Still Waters • Exploration // trap-gauntlet, no Mob count*

GM: The green wood that doesn't drift is anchored to a chest eight feet down in the deepest water, where Gators, Rayzers, and *possibly Ghost* wait. Skill-puzzle it: 2 successes (Athletics/Swim to dive, STR to rip the brickwork), one roll per stage, a failure by 10+ means a Gator gets a free bite. Inside: **Potion of Reptile Repose** (the Ghost-solution), an enchanted flintlock and an enchanted eyepatch (one Silver benefit each — roll on the Atlas tables, p. 26). The flintlock is the perfect "ranged answer to a Boss who punishes melee." ~15 min.

**S2-3 · Cypress Halls — The Rope Drop** *(combat)*
*Tag: Blow Up • Cypress Halls • Combat // Moderate: 2 Riff Roughers (2p) / 3 (3p) — Strong if the clock has passed Push*

Read-aloud: *Polished boards where the brick used to be, gas lamps turned up bright, a grid of hallways so regular it feels like a stage set. Balconies overhead, bunk doors along the walls. The floor rolls gently under you like a deck. And the drums — the drums are close enough now that your teeth hum. A cackle from above; something small and lit lands at your feet.*

GM: The bomb (1d6 to anyone who doesn't move) is the cue for Riff Roughers swinging down on ropes — coatimundi pirates, cutlasses, acid breath. One steps forward and **demands tribute**: gold (they have none — the Floor 1 gag) or something valuable. Let them talk their way past with a trade if they've been clever, or fight. Optional spice: **Shadowfall** — a wrong brick kills the lanterns, gas fills the hall over two rounds; kill or drive off the Roughers in two rounds or they light it. That's a clock-drainer *and* a Cliffhanger if you're short on time. ~40 min.

**S2-4 · B-B-B-Boss Battle — The Jazz Hall** *(combat, boss)*
*Tag: Blow Up • Cypress Halls • BOSS // Hide-Hitter Crib Daddy (L7) + 2 Trash Princesses (2p) / 3 (3p) + Scat Thug audience (2–3, side depends on the fork)*

Use the jazz-hall map on p. 28 (or generate a "Building" map in the Builder). Stage at the far end; drum kit on it; risers with Princesses; tables with Scat Thugs; the bar along one wall; a kitchen hatch (escape route / a place to throw someone).

Read-aloud (entrance): *The doors open on a New Orleans jazz hall someone dropped into a sewer. Tables, a bar, a stage — and on it, behind a drum kit the size of a car, an enormous raccoon in a blue fedora and a red tie and nothing else, laying down a groove so good it's insulting. The music stops. Time stops with it. A menu unfolds in the air — and your names are on it, priced by the pound.*

Then the AI's announcement (your own words — hype it like a wrestling entrance), then *the beat comes back faster*.

**Round-by-round frame:**
- **Round 1:** Princesses run interference — they body-block the stage; Crib Daddy opens with **Downbeat** (30ft burst, Fatigued) to slow the party. Trash Princesses within 10ft of him get +1d4 damage but must roll 1d6; on a 1 they hit each other. Say this out loud the first time it happens — it's the clue.
- **When they use the weakness:** anything that *takes his attention* — a thrown object at the kit, a spell that makes noise, a crawler climbing the risers, an insult that lands (CHA Check vs 13+F) — costs him his next drum attack as he lunges to protect the sticks/kit. **Disarm** (STR 13+F to grab the sticks, or break them) and he's down to club/dagger/spear until a Scat Thug fetches a new pair (2 rounds). Kill him *while* distracted → *Distracted Daddy* → Gold Asshole Box (read it deadpan).
- **If Driscoll is with them:** round 2 he kicks the doors, the musk hits, and the Princesses spend the fight chasing *him* around the tables. The Scat Thugs at the tables **turn** on the Boss. Crib Daddy is alone on stage.
- **If Selina + Ghost are with them:** Ghost comes through the kitchen hatch. Immune to the Princesses' area attacks? No — but 7th level and hungry. The Scat Thugs fight the party.
- **Never let them melee him for free:** every Amazing melee hit splashes musk → Sepsis. Say it before the first melee attack, not after.
- **Scale on the fly:** if it's a rout, a Princess frenzy targets the party's healer; if it's a wipe, the AI "helpfully" points out the drumsticks. Target band: sweating, not dead.

**Aftermath:** Bronze Boss Box + **Neighborhood Map** (Bogbricks and all its Mobs on the minimap; +1 hr grinding credit). Everyone gains 1 Level (Neighborhood Boss). The drums stop — and for the first time the party hears the water, and, from far off, a different beat from the next Neighborhood. Off-ramp to the liminal corridor.

**Break points for Session 2:** end on the Boss (the Smaller Goal is the box-opening in the saferoom — let them open it at the table). If time runs out at S2-3, **Shadowfall is the Cliffhanger**: lights out, gas hissing, cutlasses in the dark.

**Ghost** has no stat block in the write-up (he's a Level 7 crawler, not a Mob). If someone insists on fighting him, borrow the Crib Daddy's line (Level 7, 14+F Evade, DR 1, ~8 Health Bars of 4) with *Bite 14+F, 2d6+3 Piercing, Sepsis on Major Fail* and *Tail 13+F, 1d8+3 Bludgeoning, Take Down*, Swim ×2 speed. Better: let the Potion of Reptile Repose be the answer.

## 5. People (NPC cards)

- **Selina Poe Deveraux** — Crawler #9,329,440, L5, Human, no class yet. Marine biologist who followed her study-alligator into the dungeon. *Want:* keep Ghost alive. *Friction:* hides what Ghost is; jumpy, will fight any Mob near the water. *Tell:* hums with the drums. Gear: magic spear, net, Enchanted Pet Carrier, Enhanced Pet Biscuit, 3 Potions of Healing to trade. Survives the Neighborhood if allied → a returning mentor on Floor 2 who knows animals.
- **Mad Driscoll, the Scat Hero** — L6 NPC, Scat Thug folk hero, one-legged (Ghost took it). *Want:* Ghost dead. *Friction:* throws bombs without warning; won't share the sword until Ghost is dead. *Tell:* taps the peg-leg to the beat and hates it. Grants: enchanted longsword (2 Silver bonuses), Longsword Rank 1 (2 hrs), Scat Thug ceasefire, Boss-chamber defection. Killing him = *Reality Sets In* (Gold Mythbuster Box).
- **Ghost** — Crawler #9,329,439, L7, Alligator. Non-verbal; used to humans; likes meat and one woman's voice. Docile for up to 60 hours on a Potion of Reptile Repose.
- **Gordo the Bopca** (Still Waters saferoom) — surly, smells, gives one true warning: *"He can't leave the hall. His girls can."*

## 6. Secrets & Clues

1. **The tempo is a compass** — faster toward Cypress Halls; the Boss can't leave the jazz hall.
2. **He needs the sticks** — Scat Thugs steal drumsticks for him; a Scat Thug's satchel has a spare pair (that's the "something for the local Boss" on Table 2 #20).
3. **His fans hurt each other** — Trash Princesses in a frenzy near him will hit each other on a 1.
4. **Don't get close** — his stink infects on a good hit; people who fought him "won and died a day later" (Scat Thug gossip).
5. **The green wood that doesn't drift** — a chest is anchored eight feet down in the deepest Still Waters.
6. **Mad Driscoll's musk** drives the Princesses wild — the Scat Thugs joke that it's why he's single.
7. **The Gnawtria tunnels** come up dry near saferooms and skip the water.

## 7. Rewards

- **Bronze Boss Box** + **Neighborhood Map (Bogbricks)** — Boss drop; map = minimap + Mobs + 1 bonus grind hour.
- **Bog Bait** → Silver Shoe Box (stood still in still water).
- **Where the Wild Things Are** → no box; the AI's contempt (spotted Ghost).
- **Reality Sets In** → Gold Mythbuster Box (killed Driscoll).
- **Distracted Daddy** → Gold Asshole Box (killed the Boss by distraction).
- **Driscoll's enchanted longsword** (roll 2 Silver benefits, Atlas p. 26) + **Longsword Rank 1** — condition: Ghost dies, 2 hours of training.
- **Detect Trap Rank 1** — condition: disarm a Scat Thug trap with flair and talk shop with them.
- **Buried chest**: Potion of Reptile Repose, enchanted flintlock, enchanted eyepatch (1 Silver benefit each).
- **Selina's** 3 Potions of Healing (trade) and the Enchanted Pet Carrier (if Ghost is caught).

## 8. If you only have one night

Run S1-0 → S1-2 → S1-3/S1-4 (merged: Driscoll's bomb *is* the end of the gator fight) → S2-3 → S2-4. Drop Vibrant Walls and the chest; give the party the Potion of Reptile Repose from Selina instead. That's five cards, ~2.5 hours, one break point (the fork) in the middle.
