Bogbricks I: Follow the Drums

This file carries Session Prep Builder data for Bogbricks I: Follow the Drums. Open the DCC Session Prep Builder, click Import, and choose this file. The builder will load the overview, 5 encounter cards, 7 stat blocks, 7 clues and 9 rewards; add a map on the Maps tab (the jazz hall is Toolkit p. 28) and print.
