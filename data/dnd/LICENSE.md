# D&D 2024 data layer — sourcing & attribution

This data layer powers the D&D character sheet, builder, reference pages, and GM
tools. It is built from two kinds of content:

1. **Game mechanics** — ability scores, class tables, level progressions, spell
   parameters (level, school, range, components, duration, numeric effects),
   creature statistics, and item statistics. Game mechanics and the facts that
   express them are not protected by copyright and are used freely here.

2. **Descriptive text** — written either in original wording, or drawn from the
   **System Reference Document 5.2**, which Wizards of the Coast released under
   the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC-BY-4.0)**.

Content that is **not** in the SRD (for example many subclasses, the Artificer
class, Forgotten Realms character options, and non-SRD spells, feats, monsters,
and magic items) is represented here by its mechanics plus original descriptive
wording, not by reproduction of the published books' text.
